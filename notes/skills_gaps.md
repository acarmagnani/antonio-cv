### Geography insight

The two markets want opposite things.

- Green building certification: **2/4 in Spain, 0/3 in Sweden**
- Institutional financial modeling: **1/4 in Spain, 2/3 in Sweden**
- Carbon accounting: 1/4 in Spain, 0/3 in Sweden, 1/1 in the UK posting

So the certification decision follows the country decision, not the other way round. Spain
rewards BREEAM or LEED. Sweden rewards modeling and does not ask for certifications at all.

Power BI is the only gap that appears in both (2/4 and 2/3).
