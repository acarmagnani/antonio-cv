## Anúncio 1: Internship Construction Architect (Bygningskonstruktør), Aarhus/Aalborg/Copenhagen

Internship Duration: 2 February 2027 to 25 June 2027

Do you want to work for an Architectural Firm with sustainability at the heart of its strategy? Can you contribute with solutions from your academic studies? Are you currently studying to be a Construction Architecture (Bygningskonstruktør)? Then, join us to shape a sustainable and liveable world.

### About us

Arkitema was established in 1969 making us one of Denmark's oldest architectural firms. Today, we are well positioned in Scandinavia with offices in Denmark, Norway and Sweden. In our markets we provide wide range of competencies in our areas of expertise. We cover sectors and disciplines like healthcare, industry, transformation, landscape and residential construction.

Since 2019 Arkitema has been part of COWI, one of Scandinavia's leading engineering and consultancy companies. Together with customers, partners and colleagues, we shape a future where people and societies grow and flourish. We do that by co-creating more sustainable and beautiful solutions that improve the quality of life for people today and many generations ahead.

### Create Solutions that will enable a better tomorrow

As an intern at Arkitema, you will be part of a design team in one of Scandinavia's largest and leading architectural firms, where you will work with experienced architects, construction architects, urban planners and engineers to solve a wide range of professionally relevant tasks.

Your key responsibilities will be:

- To use your academic knowledge of technical fundamentals to contribute to our projects.
- Gain valuable experience in the field of architecture and construction.

Also, you will be included in a team and assigned an experienced colleague as a mentor who will guide you throughout your internship. We carry out architecture primarily throughout the Nordic region but also other parts of the world within the areas of Housing, Learning, Business, Health, Urban, Culture and Infrastructure. Therefore, our assignments range from small preservation-worthy residential neighborhoods to complex super hospitals, bridges and cultural centers.

As an intern, you will have the opportunity to be involved in drawing the first lines, registering old building masses, supervising construction sites on ongoing projects, or modeling and detailing the many complex buildings. Our professional span will give you a broad understanding of the design profession and in-depth knowledge of the workflows in the construction industry.

### Your skills. Our team. Together we design the future.

The first step to success in this role is that you are eager to collaborate with the people around you, whether they are colleagues, partners, or customers. Developing ties with others is something you do by acting respectfully and delivering on your promises. And you never get set in your ways but keep exploring new insights and ways to improve.

On top of that, you'll have:

- Actively pursuing a bachelor's degree in architectural technology and construction management (Bygningskonstruktør).
- Have good problem-solving skills.
- Be a creative and technical thinker.
- Be able to demonstrate evidence of research and information gathering skills.

### Professional development

We strive to develop talented interns who graduate with a high level of professional skills. As part of the internship at Arkitema, there are ongoing presentations where our own specialists share their experience and best practices in topics such as sustainability, materials and reuse; BIM, Revit, design and sketching; client consultancy and tendering; restoration and building preservation; competitions and visualizations; descriptions and quality assurance; urban planning.

### Contact

If you have any unanswered questions about the role, please reach out to Jacob Faaborg-Simonsen, jafs@arkitema.com, for Aarhus/Aalborg and Adrian Balch Petersen, adpn@arkitema.com, for Copenhagen.

## Anúncio 2: Lead Architectural Project Manager, data centres (qualquer escritório escandinavo)

Data centres play a vital role in society, and the decisions made today will shape the digital and sustainable infrastructure of tomorrow. At Arkitema, we are intensifying our focus on data centres and strengthening our capabilities to support clients in delivering the next generation of digital infrastructure.

Therefore, we are looking for a Lead Architectural Project Manager to join us. In this role, you will work closely with clients, colleagues, and partners to shape and deliver data centre projects from early concept through to design and implementation. From day one, you will engage directly with clients, leading meetings, guiding decision-making, and driving the delivery of CSA disciplines across projects. You will translate global concepts into locally compliant solutions while structuring teams and workflows to ensure efficient execution.

Arkitema was established in 1969 making us one of Denmark's oldest architectural firms. Today, we are well positioned in Scandinavia with offices in Denmark, Norway and Sweden. In our markets we provide wide range of competencies in our areas of expertise. We cover sectors and disciplines like healthcare, industry, transformation, landscape and residential construction. Since 2019 Arkitema has been part of COWI, one of Scandinavia's leading engineering and consultancy companies.

### Lead multidisciplinary design for mission-critical projects

As Lead Architectural Project Manager you will provide overall design leadership. You will align client requirements, technical disciplines and operational priorities, and help teams navigate complexity without losing sight of what creates value, throughout all project phases from concept to handover.

Your key responsibilities will be to:

- Lead multidisciplinary design delivery for data centre projects.
- Ensure coordinated delivery across a large multidisciplinary project team, including discipline leads and international engineering teams.
- Set clear direction, create clarity, and facilitate effective collaboration.
- Oversee project financials, reporting to steering committees, and proactive risk management.

### Your Skills. Our Team. Together We Design The Future.

The first step to success in this role is that you are eager to collaborate with the people around you, colleagues, partners and customers. You act respectfully, listen actively and follow through on your commitments. And you remain curious about new perspectives and ways to improve.

On top of that, you will have:

- A relevant educational background in project management, architecture, engineering or a related field.
- Experience working with engineering consultancy, technical advisory or complex infrastructure projects.
- Experience from data centre projects or other large-scale, mission-critical facilities.
- Experience leading large, multidisciplinary project teams.
- Professional proficiency in English, verbal and written.

### Work location

The role can be based in any of our offices across Scandinavia. Norway: Oslo, Trondheim or Stavanger. Sweden: Stockholm or Gothenburg. Denmark: Copenhagen or Aarhus. We work in international and multidisciplinary teams, and many of our projects are delivered across offices and borders.
