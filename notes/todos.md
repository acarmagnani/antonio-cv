## Credentials, in order of impact (decided 2026-08)

1. **Real estate financial modelling course** (A.CRE, REFM, or Break Into CRE). Two to three months, low cost. This is the single highest-return item on the list: it closes the DCF and waterfall gap, which is the actual reason an investment analyst screen cuts me today.
2. **Underwrite one real asset end to end** and put it on the CV as a project. That gives me a bullet with IRR and equity multiple that is honestly mine, instead of borrowing the vocabulary.
3. **Any title with "Analyst" in it.** Not one of my seven roles says analyst. That costs me on every analyst opening.
4. **RICS APC**, through an employer that sponsors it (the valuation and advisory graduate schemes at CBRE, JLL, Cushman, Colliers, Savills are built around this route).
5. **CFA Level I**, optional. Cheap signal of finance seriousness, about nine months, only worth it if 1 and 2 are already done.

Deprioritised, and why:

- **BREEAM, LEED, VERDE, DGNB**: lowest-paying branch of everything I am considering, a technical compliance service with a low ceiling. Not a career direction.
- **GRESB and CRREM instead**, if I want the sustainability-plus-finance hybrid. Those are the acronyms investors actually read. EU Taxonomy Article 8 too.
- **ISO 14001**: no evidence any posting I have applied to asks for it.
