# Antonio — context for tailoring and job-fit

This file holds the personal context the CV/cover-letter/job-fit workflows depend on
(work eligibility, ambitions, career arc). It lives in the repo on purpose, so it travels
with the project across machines. It complements the "About Antonio" section in `CLAUDE.md`,
which covers his professional domains.

Keep it current: when timing, eligibility, or direction changes, edit this file.

## Job search 2026

Antonio is actively job-hunting.

**Work eligibility:** he holds **Italian citizenship** (EU free movement, so no visa/sponsorship needed anywhere in the EU/EEA, e.g. Spain, Denmark, Sweden). Post-Brexit this does NOT grant UK work rights: UK roles need a Skilled Worker visa + employer sponsorship. He is willing to relocate. **Spanish is fluent.**

**Direction: two horizons, and they must not be collapsed into one.**

**Long term, the target (where he wants to end up):** real estate. An investment, valuation and advisory, development, or research seat, using sustainability and data as the things that make him unusual inside that seat rather than as the job itself. This is the bullseye and what the `real-estate-investment` block is built for.

**Short and medium term, what he is actually applying to right now:** and he is genuinely willing to take adjacent work. ESG and sustainability consulting, management consulting, circular-economy and computational roles in architecture (the GXN kind), energy analyst positions, infrastructure. Getting inside a company he can then move within is a realistic route to the target, so an energy analyst seat at a developer is a good outcome, not a detour.

**What this means for a verdict on a posting:** the long-term ranking is about DESTINATION, never a filter on which postings to send. Do not tell him to skip a solid adjacent role because it is not real estate. Reserve "Pula" for: not eligible (UK without sponsorship), several levels above him, or a role that would genuinely trap him somewhere he does not want with no way out. **"Not real estate" is not by itself a reason to skip.** When a role is adjacent rather than on-target, say so in the Futuro line and name the path from it toward real estate, instead of discouraging the application.

Two things that are settled regardless of horizon:

- **Green-certification (BREEAM/LEED/DGNB) is not a career destination.** It is the lowest-paying branch of everything he is considering, a technical compliance service with a low ceiling. Taking a job that includes certification work is fine; building a career identity around the certificate is not. For the sustainability-plus-finance hybrid, **GRESB, CRREM and EU Taxonomy Article 8 are worth more than a BREEAM assessor licence**, because those are the ones investors read.
- **The seat matters more than the subject.** An investment or asset management role with sustainability fluency pays roughly twice what a sustainability specialist inside the same firm does, because the seat carries deal responsibility. When both exist, aim for the investment side.

What his CV can actually clear today, not what he deserves. Note that his odds are HIGHEST in the adjacent families, not in the target one, which is exactly why the short-term stance above matters.

**On-target (real estate), realistic, apply in volume:** valuation and advisory graduate or analyst roles at CBRE, JLL, Cushman, Colliers, Savills (they hire built-environment graduates on purpose and train them toward the RICS APC); pricing and valuation analyst at proptech (his house-price model is direct evidence and rare in this profile); real estate research analyst; development analyst at a developer.

**On-target, underdog but worth a shot:** ESG lead inside a real estate investment manager; junior asset management at a smaller fund or family office.

**On-target, unlikely with the CV as it stands:** investment or acquisitions analyst at Genesta, NREP/Urban Partners, Greystar, Hines, Patrizia, AXA IM (they screen for finance degrees and run modelling tests he would not pass today); EY-Parthenon transaction real estate (strategy-house recruiting from target schools). Worth an application anyway when the posting is junior, but expect silence.

**Adjacent, and where his odds are genuinely best right now:** ESG and sustainability consulting in the built environment (Ramboll, Sweco, NIRAS, COWI, Bureau Veritas, Big 4 sustainability); climate risk and infrastructure advisory; energy and decarbonisation analyst roles, including inside developers and asset owners; circular-economy and computational roles in architecture and engineering (the GXN kind). Here he is a strong candidate rather than a stretch: Ramboll, Sweco, the master's, LCA, EU Taxonomy and Python all line up directly. **These are good applications, and an energy or sustainability analyst seat inside a real-estate-owning company is one of the better realistic routes toward the target.**

**The decisive gaps for the on-target roles** are experience and credential problems, not CV-writing problems: no transaction experience, no DCF or waterfall modelling, and the finance vocabulary (IRR, NPV, cap rate, yield, equity multiple, LTV) appears nowhere in his record. Closing them, in order of impact: a real estate financial modelling course (A.CRE, REFM, Break Into CRE), then underwriting one real asset end to end as a portfolio project, then any title with "Analyst" in it, then the RICS APC through an employer that sponsors it. None of this blocks applying to the adjacent families today.

## Antonio's ambitions (the "future" axis)

Antonio wants to work at the intersection of the built environment / real estate, data and
technology, and sustainability / ESG, doing strategic and analytical work: the upstream
"why / where / how could it be better" decisions, rather than execution-level architecture
(drafting, BIM, site delivery).

These are ranked by DESTINATION as of 2026-08. The ranking says where he wants to end up, not which postings are worth sending. See the two-horizon note under "Job search 2026": in the short term he applies well outside rank 1.

1. **Real estate, first choice.** Investment, valuation and advisory, development, research, pricing. Feasibility, underwriting, asset due diligence. This is the seat he wants and the one the `real-estate-investment` block is built for.
2. **Real estate + data.** Pricing, valuation modelling, proptech (Clikalia). The nearest adjacent track, and the one where his CV is most differentiated rather than deficient.
3. **ESG / sustainability + data.** EU Taxonomy, non-financial reporting, data-driven sustainability (EY CCaSS), climate risk, circular economy. As a DESTINATION this ranks third, because it pays least and has no carry. As a place to work next it ranks high, because it is where he is most employable today and it keeps every door open.

The through-line he wants: sustainability and data as the things that make him unusual inside a real estate seat, not as the job description itself.

He is open across seniority levels, including analyst and other entry/junior roles, when they are a genuine entry rung. He has finished his studies, so he does not pursue internships, but he applies that filter himself.

Geography, and here too the horizons are separate.

**Short term, what he is searching NOW.** He is not looking to leave Europe at this point. Spain and Italy are the furthest he would move today. Within that, role type matters more than which of these countries it is: a good seat in Stockholm beats a mediocre one in Copenhagen. Priority order as of 2026-08:

1. **The Nordics, as one bloc.** Denmark and Copenhagen come first because he already lives there: no relocation cost or delay, an existing network. But the priority is the region, not the country. Malmö is 35 minutes away and commutable without moving at all. Stockholm, Gothenburg, Oslo and Helsinki are all genuine options he would relocate for. Search in English throughout. Danish real estate specifically stays weak (small, domestic, runs in Danish), but ESG, LCA/DGNB and construction-adjacent roles are viable.
2. **Spain (Madrid/Barcelona).** Professionally the most interesting market of all: more investment funds, bigger consulting offices, CSRD/EU Taxonomy driving ESG advisory demand, green certifications (BREEAM/LEED) actually valued, and he is fluent in Spanish. He is not there yet, which is the only reason it sits second. Strong parallel track, searched daily.
3. **London.** Passive. Post-Brexit the Italian passport gives no UK work rights, so only sponsored roles are open.
4. **Milan / Italy.** Long-term interest, career and personal, but he does not speak Italian yet. Opportunistic only, for English-speaking international teams.
5. **Occasional sweep: Netherlands, Switzerland.** Worth a look every few days rather than daily. EU eligibility is fine; the open question is how much is posted in English.

Portugal ruled out (weak market for his target roles).

**Long term, open but NOT what he is looking for now.** He is not closed to Brazil, the United States, or Asia, and would consider any of them later for something genuinely exceptional. For Brazil specifically, the compensation case is real (same market and cost base as his benchmark, native Portuguese, PJ taxation around 11-15% against roughly 45% in Denmark). But none of these should be surfaced as a current option or counted as a reason to prefer one posting over another today. If a remarkable opportunity in one of them appears, mention it as exactly that: remarkable, and out of the current search.

## Antonio's journey (the storytelling spine)

Antonio trained and worked as an architect in Brazil and is genuinely passionate about the built environment, real estate, and its financial and market side (why things get built, how the market moves). Alongside architecture he has two long-standing interests: data (using it to understand what is built and what could be built better) and environment / ESG / sustainability.

He was drawn away from architecture's execution focus (client wishes, design, delivery) toward the upstream, strategic questions it usually skips: why is this being built here, where and how could it be done better. To bring real estate, data, sustainability, and that strategic lens together, he moved from Brazil to Denmark for the Master's Degree in Architecture Computation at The Royal Danish Academy, whose aim is to use data, technology, and innovation to improve real estate and
construction.

This journey is the raw material for cover-letter storytelling, and this file is now its canonical home. The pre-written openings in `letter_base.yaml` are the letter-ready renderings of it, one per job family. (The old `cover_letter_base.md` held a duplicate; it has been retired to `archive/`.)
