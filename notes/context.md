## Antonio's journey (the storytelling spine)

Antonio trained and worked as an architect in Brazil and is genuinely passionate about the built environment, real estate, and its financial and market side (why things get built, how the market moves). Alongside architecture he has two long-standing interests: data (using it to understand what is built and what could be built better) and environment / ESG / sustainability.

He was drawn away from architecture's execution focus (client wishes, design, delivery) toward the upstream, strategic questions it usually skips: why is this being built here, where and how could it be done better. To bring real estate, data, sustainability, and that strategic lens together, he moved from Brazil to Denmark for the M.Arch in Architecture Computation at The Royal Danish Academy, whose aim is to use data, technology, and innovation to improve real estate and
construction.
