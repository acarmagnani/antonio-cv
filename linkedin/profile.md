### Notes on both

**On "Architecture Computation":** it stays, but in the credibility slot at the end next to
Royal Danish Academy, never in the identity slot at the front. It is the real name of the
degree and a genuine differentiator, but it matches no search anyone runs and means nothing to
a recruiter scanning for three seconds.

**On naming Sweco and Ramboll when neither is current:** legitimate. Daniel names BIG in his
headline and left BIG in 2021. That slot is a credibility stack, not a statement of current
employment.

**On headline truncation:** LinkedIn cuts the headline around 60-70 characters on mobile and in
search results, so the first segment has to stand alone. Both variants are built that way.

**On not reading as a copy of Daniel's profile:** three phrases were near-verbatim in an earlier
draft and are deliberately changed. His "On the side, I build ... for AEC firms" is now "I also
build tools for ...". His "what I actually care about is the moment ..." pivot is now "I like
the part where ...". His closing "Based in Copenhagen." is now "Copenhagen based, ...". Keep
those three clear on any future edit; the rest of the structure is common LinkedIn practice, not
his signature.

**On buzzword density:** an earlier draft stacked six frameworks into one About sentence (UNGP,
CSRD, EU Taxonomy, LCA, climate risk, HRDD) and eight terms into the headline. Both are trimmed
to the two or three that carry weight. The rest live in the skills list, which is what LinkedIn
search actually indexes, so nothing is lost by keeping them out of prose.
