The Opportunity

Join our Project Management team in Madrid and gain hands-on experience supporting the coordination and delivery of exciting projects. You'll have the opportunity to develop your skills in project planning, cost management, and client communication, working alongside experienced professionals.

 

As a Project Management Consultant at Arup, you will:

·       Support project coordination, working closely with technical teams to ensure efficient project delivery.

·       Contribute to the development of bids, reports, and other project documentation.

·       Assist in project monitoring, financial control, and cost management.

·       Participate in client and supplier meetings, gaining valuable communication and stakeholder management experience.

·       Contribute to quality control processes, ensuring the highest standards of project delivery.

 

Does this role inspire you?
