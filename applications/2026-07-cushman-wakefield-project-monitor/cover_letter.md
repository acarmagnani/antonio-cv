Dear Hiring Team at Cushman & Wakefield,

I trained as an architect, but the part of the work that pulled me hardest was never the drawing board, it was the layer underneath it, whether a project's assumptions about cost, schedule, and risk actually hold once construction starts. Cushman & Wakefield's work monitoring commercial real estate projects for institutional investors across office, retail, logistics, and mixed-use sits exactly on that layer, and it's where I want to put my architecture background and analytical training to work.

At Sweco, I support the delivery of internationally financed water and energy infrastructure projects, work that runs on the same discipline this role asks for. I authored climate risk assessments for major water projects under JASPERS and EBRD guidance, rating hazard exposure asset by asset, joined a field mission across Baku's water supply system to assess technical and environmental risk through site inspections, and reviewed engineering designs against subconsultants' technical drawings under FIDIC contracts. I also built a pricing tool that stress-tested project economics against fee bands and delivery scenarios before bid decisions were made. It is the same job a project monitor does for a developer or investor, reading a project's technical and financial exposure and turning it into something a client can act on.

That instinct for coordination and compliance carries back to my time at Itaú-Unibanco, where I reviewed technical documentation on new office towers under construction at the bank's São Paulo headquarters and assessed the environmental and energy performance of buildings the bank was acquiring or leasing as part of internal due diligence, and to Isay Weinfeld, where I coordinated structural, HVAC, electrical, and lighting inputs into a single Revit model. Both taught me how many disciplines a single asset has to satisfy before it is actually delivered.

Cushman & Wakefield's reach across sectors and institutional clients is what draws me, real projects with real capital behind them, and the chance to be the person who keeps their delivery honest.

All the best,
