# Cushman & Wakefield — Project Manager / Project Monitor, Commercial Real Estate (Milan)

## Job Description Summary

Cushman & Wakefield is a leading global commercial real estate services firm, providing expert solutions in property management, investment, leasing, valuation, and workplace strategy. With operations in more than 60 countries and a strong focus on innovation, sustainability, and client success, the company helps businesses optimize their real estate portfolios and create spaces where people and ideas thrive.

This role offers the opportunity to work on complex and high-profile real estate developments across office, retail, logistics, hospitality, and mixed-use sectors, supporting institutional investors, developers, and international stakeholders throughout the entire project lifecycle.

## Job Description

The ideal candidate combines strong analytical and organizational capabilities with a genuine interest in real estate, project delivery, and urban development, gaining direct exposure to strategic real estate.

### Key Responsibilities

- Support the coordination and monitoring of commercial real estate projects from planning through completion
- Track project timelines, budgets, milestones, and overall performance
- Coordinate with developers, architects, engineers, contractors, consultants, and other stakeholders
- Prepare periodic project monitoring reports and presentations for clients and stakeholders
- Support due diligence and feasibility analysis for real estate investments and developments
- Identify project risks, delays, and budget variances, proposing mitigation strategies
- Track CAPEX deployment, cash flows, and project financial indicators
- Ensure compliance with planning, permitting, ESG, HSE, and contractual requirements
- Participate in project meetings and manage communication among project parties
- Support procurement and tendering activities where applicable
- Contribute to continuous improvement of project monitoring and reporting processes

### Requirements

- Degree in Engineering, Architecture, or related disciplines
- 3–5 years of experience in architecture or MEP consultancy, project management, construction, or related fields
- Strong analytical, organizational, and communication skills
- Excellent interpersonal and communication abilities
- Proactive attitude and ability to work effectively in collaborative environments
- Excellent command of Microsoft Excel and PowerPoint
- Fluency in English is required; additional languages are considered a plus

### What We Offer

- Exposure to high-profile commercial real estate projects
- Dynamic and international working environment
- Direct interaction with leading market participants and institutional clients
- Professional growth and development opportunities
- Collaborative and performance-driven culture
- Flexible working arrangements
