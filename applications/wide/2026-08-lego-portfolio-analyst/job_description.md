# LEGO, Portfolio Analyst, Engineering & Quality Strategic Projects & Portfolio Management, Billund

This Portfolio Analyst role sits in Engineering & Quality Strategic Projects & Portfolio Management in Billund, with no relocation support offered. You'll turn portfolio data into clear choices for senior leaders.

## Play your part in our team succeeding

Our team guides a varied portfolio of strategic initiatives across Engineering & Quality, from sustainability projects to new technologies and play experiences. You'll help the LEGO team see where resources, budgets, risks and benefits connect, so the right work moves forward with clarity. Together, we'll explore, experiment and learn.

## Core Responsibilities

**Portfolio Data Management & Analysis.** Collect, structure, maintain and evaluate comprehensive data on the project portfolio, including resources, budgets, risks, benefits, delivery performance and others. Support scenario analysis and prioritization models, e.g. project intake process.

**Performance Tracking.** Develop, maintain and continuously improve project portfolio performance dashboards and reporting tools to track key portfolio metrics and enable data-driven decisions.

**Decision Support.** Create clear, concise decision-making materials with compelling narratives tailored to senior management, enabling well-informed decisions. Communicate complex topics in a simple, easily understandable manner.

**Operational & Strategic Support.** Take ownership of ad-hoc portfolio analysis, reporting, and other operational tasks where needed. Identify and proactively drive opportunities to streamline processes, enhance workflows, and improve operational efficiency.

**Governance & Decision Framework.** Support and enforce governance structures, decision-making processes, and portfolio management standards that ensure accountability, consistency, and transparency.
