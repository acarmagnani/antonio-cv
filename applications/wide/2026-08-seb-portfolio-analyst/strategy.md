# Strategy — SEB Alternatives, Portfolio Analyst (Portfolio Analysis and Operations)

**Positioning:** an analyst who builds the Excel systems other people run on. The posting asks for a strong analytical foundation, Excel proficiency and Excel-based tools, initiative, and independence, and is explicit that private equity experience is beneficial rather than required. That is the opening. Antonio does not compete on finance credentials, so the application competes on demonstrated analytical rigor, on tools he built rather than spreadsheets he filled in, and on having worked inside a bank already.

**Profile chosen:** `profile.proptech-real-estate`. It opens on real estate development and financial analysis, and names financial feasibility modeling and machine learning applied to property and market data. That reads to a finance team as quantitative credibility. The alternative (`profile.real-estate`) ends its hands-on list on volumetric studies in Revit, which is noise for a portfolio analyst.

**Threads to lead with:**

1. **The Colt risk system at Ramboll.** A fully automated risk-assessment system in Excel, consolidating risk data across supply chains into a single dashboard that updated dynamically. This is the anchor. It is the closest thing he has to portfolio reconciliation and reporting, an Excel-based tool that keeps a moving picture accurate, and it answers the Excel requirement with something built rather than used.
2. **OSPA, investment numbers.** 15+ feasibility studies across Brazil and the US, sizing investment and return against buildable area, unit mix, price per square metre, and parking to test viability, then presenting the outcomes to developer clients. Financial analysis plus client-facing reporting, the two halves of this job.
3. **Itaú-Unibanco, inside a bank.** A VBA-powered Excel dashboard replacing a manual inventory process across locations, and due diligence review on buildings the bank was acquiring or leasing. Shows he has worked in a banking environment and has automated an operational process end to end.

**Gaps not to paper over:** no private equity, no fund accounting, no reconciliation of capital calls or distributions. The letter names the gap once, briefly, and moves. It does not claim finance credentials he does not have. SEB Alternatives also runs real estate strategies, which is the honest bridge between his background and their portfolio.
