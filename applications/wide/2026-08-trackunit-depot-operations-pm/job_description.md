# Product Manager — Depot Operations (Rental Pod) — Trackunit

Come make a difference in a fast-growing, innovative, Software Product Management team that combines a scale-up atmosphere with a global industry-leading product portfolio. You'll be part of building the future of Trackunit's connected ecosystem - helping shape products that connect people, data, and machines to build a smarter construction industry.

At Trackunit, we put our customers first. As a Product Manager, you'll work across teams and departments to guide software products from research and idea through to launch - connecting the customer, technical, and business worlds. You'll break down complex problems into clear, actionable steps and drive product development with focus, purpose, and measurable outcomes.

In this area, we focus on the people running day to day depot operations, getting equipment out to jobsites and back in again, checked, ready and accounted for. There's significant potential here, and this role gives you the chance to shape a product experience purpose built for this persona from the ground up.

As Product Manager, you will own the roadmap and priorities that help depot teams manage equipment flowing in and out smoothly and reliably. You will work closely with Engineering, Design and Go to Market teams to turn a broad and still evolving problem space into real product value for our customers.

You will be connected to one of our offices in Europe or North America. For this position, you will need to be based in Europe or in the Eastern time zone in North America. No relocation assistance.

## Who are you ideally?

- You're comfortable in your product management craft and have experience taking a product from zero to one, defining scope and direction in an ambiguous space rather than executing against an already defined roadmap.
- You're skilled at getting close to users through direct discovery, and can translate what you learn from depot teams into a clear product direction.
- Big plus if you've worked with data heavy products and are sharp at translating machine and operational data into clear, actionable decisions for the people running the depot day to day.
- You think and build AI-first. You reach for AI capabilities as a default design tool, not a bolt-on, and you know how to evaluate when AI genuinely reduces friction versus when it adds noise. You've shipped or closely partnered on AI-powered product features.
- You bring great structuring and analytical skills with the ability to implement strategic product plans and to proactively make things happen with a hands-on attitude.
- You're an effective communicator who knows how to work asynchronously and across time zones.
- You're experienced in the full product lifecycle: from backlog refinement and sprint execution to coordinating with go-to-market teams.
- You're adept at navigating trade-offs, bringing cross-functional clarity, and moving work forward.
- You bring commercial awareness and know how to define and measure progress with data and feedback loops.
- You focus on delivering impact, not just output, and you own what you build.

Don't meet every single requirement? No worries - if you're super excited about this role but your past experience doesn't align perfectly with every qualification, we encourage you to apply.

## Primary Responsibilities

- Own the direction for this area: Define what gets built and why, so that depot teams can rely on clear data and workflows to keep equipment moving in and out smoothly.
- Drive end-to-end product execution: Own the build process from backlog refinement to release, working closely with Engineering and Design to deliver high-quality outcomes.
- Lead go-to-market efforts: Coordinate with Marketing, Sales, and Customer Success.
- Create clarity across functions: Align teams on scope, trade-offs, and timelines.
- Build effective async collaboration across time zones and stakeholders.
- Shape how we work: Contribute to evolving product practices, frameworks, and rituals.
