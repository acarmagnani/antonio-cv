# Data & Analytics Engineer

**Company:** Hobbii (hired via external recruiter, "On Behalf of Hobbii")
**Location:** Copenhagen, DK
**Company language:** English

## About Hobbii

Hobbii is Denmark's fastest-growing e-commerce company within yarn and handicrafts. From our
base in Copenhagen, we've built a global brand serving passionate makers in more than 12
markets, with an ambition to become the world's leading online yarn universe. Driven by
scalable technology, creative energy, and data-driven commerce, we're turning our passion for
craftsmanship into a global success story.

This is a rare chance to join a small, dedicated data team at Hobbii and turn raw, complex data
into trusted, business-ready answers the whole company relies on.

## About the role

You'll work with stakeholders across Finance, Product, and Tech, serving the business with
their data needs and helping users make the most of our customer-centric data. You'll have full
ownership of your data products end to end, as well as talking to and educating the business.

## Key responsibilities

- Work closely with Finance, Product and Commercial to understand what they need, and translate
  it into clear, trustworthy answers
- Own shared KPI definitions such as AOV, CLV, and Margin in our Cube semantic layer, so the
  business measures things consistently
- Contribute to maintaining and building SQL models and dashboards, and help key stakeholders
  understand the implementation
- Build useful Metabase dashboards and utilise coding and AI tools to help answer business
  questions effectively
- Contribute to the Data Dictionary so non-technical users understand what they are measuring
  and can explore data with confidence

## Your profile

You've probably been the data person somewhere small before, so you're used to owning things
end to end and figuring them out yourself. You're as curious about the business as the tech,
you'd rather answer a real question than build something clever no one asked for, and you're
comfortable talking to stakeholders.

We imagine you bring:

- Genuine business curiosity and the instinct to translate stakeholder questions into practical
  solutions, this matters as much as the tech
- Experience with a modern data warehouse such as Redshift
- Interest in semantic modelling, KPI logic, or reusable business definitions. Cube, dbt, or
  LookML experience is a plus
- Curiosity about using AI and MCP for SQL support, documentation, or data exploration
- English fluency, written and spoken, our company language
- A relevant degree or equivalent practical background
