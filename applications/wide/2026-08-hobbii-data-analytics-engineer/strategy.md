# Strategy, Hobbii Data & Analytics Engineer

## Positioning

Antonio is the person who has repeatedly been the only data person in the room: he spots the
manual process or the unanswered business question, builds the tool end to end, and then gets
colleagues to actually use it.

## Profile chosen

`profile.proptech`. It is the most data-forward variant available (Python, machine learning,
"building data tools that turn spatial and financial data into decisions") and ends on
technology and data rather than on ESG or infrastructure.

**Known weakness:** every profile variant anchors to real estate and the built environment,
which is a mismatch for e-commerce. A dedicated data/analytics profile variant should be
authored into `content_base.yaml` with Antonio if he applies to more roles of this kind.

## Threads to lead with

1. **Built the tool, then drove adoption.** The Grasshopper staircase plugin at Isay Weinfeld
   is the anchor: Python, days to 20 minutes, then a studio workshop and adoption across 10+
   projects. That is the JD's "full ownership of your data products end to end, as well as
   talking to and educating the business", in a different industry. The Itau VBA dashboard
   (replaced a manual process) and the Ramboll automated Excel risk dashboard are the same
   pattern at two other companies.

2. **Business curiosity over clever tech.** The JD says this matters as much as the tech. Use
   OSPA feasibility (investment and return against unit mix and price per square metre), the
   Sweco pricing model (fee bands and project economics for viability testing), and the Ramboll
   critical raw materials study (regulatory drivers turned into business development targets).
   These show he reaches for the business question, not the elegant model.

3. **Small-team ownership.** VIZU (co-founded, 10+ clients, scoping through delivery, hiring)
   is the literal evidence for "you've probably been the data person somewhere small before".

4. **Structured data and modelling depth.** The house price prediction project (ensemble
   learning on structured property and financial data, top 2%) is the strongest pure-data
   credential. The embodied carbon toolkit carries the documentation-for-adoption angle that
   maps onto the Data Dictionary responsibility.

## Gap to handle honestly in the letter

No production data warehouse, no Redshift, dbt, Cube, or Metabase. Do not claim them. The
honest move is to name the transferable core (SQL, Python, semantic and definitional thinking
built through KPI and scoring logic in his own tools) and state plainly that the specific stack
is learnable, without over-apologising or dwelling on it.
