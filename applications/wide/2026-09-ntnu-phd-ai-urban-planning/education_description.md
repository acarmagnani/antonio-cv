## Master's degree, for the 2000-character field (1994 characters)

Master of Arts (MA) in Architecture (cand.arch.), programme: Computation in Architecture. The Royal Danish Academy - Architecture, Design, Conservation, Copenhagen, a state-recognised institution under the Danish Ministry of Higher Education and Science; the programme was accredited by the Danish Accreditation Council in 2010. Two years full time, 120 ECTS, NQF/EQF level 7 (Bologna second cycle), taught and examined entirely in English. Admission required a completed bachelor's degree in architecture. Completed 26 May 2026.

Computation in Architecture is the Academy's computational and data-driven track within the architecture master's. Teaching is project-based: each module is carried by independent work developed against a research question, using programming, geometry, simulation, material experimentation and data analysis. Modules: Relational Strategies: Performance/Making/Modelling (30 ECTS); Tactical Experimentations: Research Informed Design Inquiry (30 ECTS); Synthetic Constructions A and B: Critically Informed Design Practice (20 and 10 ECTS); and the thesis project (30 ECTS).

The work covered computational geometry and parametric modelling, environmental and structural simulation, material systems with life-cycle assessment, machine learning on urban and property data, and geospatial analysis in QGIS. My thesis, Computational Urban Analysis for Intensification Strategy, built a framework identifying underused space in central São Paulo by combining urban morphology, land use data and social vulnerability indicators, generating intensification typologies against existing municipal policy. A parallel project produced a machine learning tool recommending site-specific development for Copenhagen buildings from financial, social, sustainability and geospatial data, with an interface letting non-technical stakeholders adjust drivers and weightings.

With the preceding bachelor's degree in architecture, the degree fulfils EU Directive 2005/36, Article 46.

## Summary against NTNU's required criterion

The required criterion asks for a relevant Master's degree in urban planning or architecture, corresponding to a five-year Norwegian Master's, of which 120 credits are obtained at master's level. The two degrees below meet that description directly: a five-and-a-half-year integrated professional degree in Architecture and Urbanism comprising 3,987 curricular hours, followed by a two-year Master of Arts in Architecture of 120 ECTS at EQF level 7. The master's programme is taught and examined entirely in English, and the diploma supplement records that, taken together with the preceding bachelor's degree in architecture, it fulfils the common European architectural qualification requirements of EU Directive 2005/36, Article 46.

## Master of Arts (MA) in Architecture, Computation in Architecture

**Institution:** The Royal Danish Academy, Architecture, Design, Conservation (Det Kongelige Akademi), Copenhagen, a state-recognised higher education institution regulated under the Danish Ministry of Higher Education and Science. The Master's Programme in Architecture was accredited by the Danish Accreditation Council in 2010.

**Qualification:** Master of Arts (MA) in Architecture, cand.arch. (candidatus architecturae). Programme name: Computation in Architecture. Completed 26 May 2026, diploma issued June 2026.

**Level and scope:** NQF/EQF level 7, second cycle in the Bologna framework. Two years of full-time study, 120 ECTS credits. Language of instruction and examination: English. Admission required a completed bachelor's degree in architecture from a state-recognised institution. The degree gives access to third-cycle (PhD) study.

**Structure, as recorded on the transcript:** Relational Strategies: Performance / Making / Modelling (30 ECTS); Tactical Experimentations: Research Informed Design Inquiry (30 ECTS); Synthetic Constructions (A): Critically Informed Design Practice (20 ECTS); Synthetic Constructions (B): Critically Informed Design Practice (10 ECTS); and the thesis project, Afgangsprojekt (30 ECTS). The diploma supplement describes the thesis as an independent, analytical, project-oriented and/or theoretical study documenting the student's ability to apply scientific theory and methodology to a clearly defined architectural subject.

**What the programme covers.** Computation in Architecture is the Royal Danish Academy's computational and data-driven track within the architecture master's, taught in the environment of the Institute of Architecture and Technology and its research culture in computational design. The teaching is project-based: each module is carried by a piece of independent work developed against a research question, with methods drawn from programming, geometry, simulation, material experimentation and data analysis rather than from studio convention. Over the two years the work covered computational geometry and parametric modelling, environmental and structural simulation, material systems and life-cycle assessment, machine learning applied to urban and property data, and geospatial analysis.

**Work produced during the programme, most relevant to this position:**

- **Thesis, Computational Urban Analysis for Intensification Strategy.** A computational framework identifying underused space in the historic centre of São Paulo, combining urban morphology analysis, land use data and social vulnerability indicators, with an algorithm that generates programmatic and morphological typologies for intensification against the city's existing urban policy, and multiple geospatial datasets integrated in QGIS to map the relationship between buildings, zoning conditions and social context at neighbourhood scale.
- **Real Estate Development Recommendation Tool.** A machine learning tool generating site-specific development recommendations for buildings in Copenhagen from financial, social, sustainability and geospatial data, with an interface allowing non-technical stakeholders to adjust the impact drivers and their weightings and see the predictions change.
- **Rammed Blue Biomass and Computational Optimisation.** A construction technique and material system using seashells and algae, developed with LCA metrics alongside parametric simulation so that structural performance and environmental impact were assessed together. The work was selected as a semester highlight and became the material basis for the school's installation at the Venice Architecture Biennale 2025, presented with CITA, GXN and 3XN at the Arsenale.
- **Computational Deployable Structures.** A two-week international workshop at Southeast University, Nanjing, for a selected group from the Royal Danish Academy, applying origami-based deployable-structure principles to architectural structures through parametric modelling.

**Grading:** the Danish 7-point scale (12, 10, 7, 4, 02, 00, -3), which the diploma supplement maps to ECTS letter grades; both appear on the transcript of records. Danish qualifications carry no overall classification, which is why none is stated on the diploma.

## Bachelor's Degree in Architecture and Urbanism

**Institution:** Universidade Presbiteriana Mackenzie, Faculdade de Arquitetura e Urbanismo, São Paulo, Brazil. The university is recognised by Federal Decree 30.511 of 1952 and re-accredited by Ministry of Education Ordinance 1.824 of 2011; the Architecture and Urbanism programme is recognised by Ministry of Education Decree 23.275 of 1947, with recognition renewed by Ordinance 111 of 4 February 2021.

**Qualification:** Bacharel em Arquitetura e Urbanismo (Bachelor's Degree in Architecture and Urbanism). Admission by entrance examination (vestibular) in August 2018, studies completed 23 December 2023, degree conferred 29 February 2024, diploma issued 4 March 2024.

**Level and scope:** the integrated five-year professional degree in architecture in the Brazilian system, and the qualification that carries professional title and the right to registration with the national council of architects. Total curricular load: **3,987 hours**, a nominal five-year length corresponding to approximately **300 ECTS** (Brazilian institutions do not issue ECTS, so this is a duration-based conversion, not a figure taken from the diploma). Teaching language: Portuguese.

**Structure.** The programme is built on four parallel strands running semester by semester, each strand examined through project work rather than through written examination alone:

- **Architectural design studios, Ateliê Projeto I to VIII**, at 158 hours each, running the length of the degree and organised by theme: culture, construction, technology, and city and theory.
- **Urbanism studios, Estúdio Urbanismo I to VIII**, at 79 hours each, covering theory and the city, society and the city, and landscape and the city. Eight consecutive semesters of urban design and planning studio work is the part of the degree most directly relevant to this position.
- **Theory and history of architecture and urbanism I to VI**, alongside critical theory of architecture, and city, culture and everyday life.
- **Technology and building science:** structural stability (physics and geometry, strength of materials and material properties), applied soil mechanics, special building systems, environmental comfort I to III (thermal, acoustic and lighting performance), topography I, and **topography II: geoprocessing**, which is where GIS enters the degree.

Alongside these: representation and expression studios, physical and virtual modelling, interior architecture, cultural heritage and retrofit techniques, methodology applied to architecture and urbanism, entrepreneurship and entrepreneurial projects, and ethics and citizenship.

**Final graduation project (TFG I and TFG II, 269 and 206 hours respectively, with individual academic supervision).** The Brazilian final graduation project is an independent, year-long, individually supervised piece of work, defended before a panel, and is the closest equivalent in the degree to a thesis.

**Complementary activities recorded on the transcript** include the Architectural Association Autumn Semester Programme 2022 and the Architectural Association Visiting School summer programme in London, an international architecture competition (Kaira Looro, Women's House), academic study trips to Copenhagen and Porto, and student research and teaching activities.

**Grading:** the Brazilian 0 to 10 scale, applied per discipline. Across the 45 graded disciplines of the degree, weighted by hours, the average is **7.85 out of 10**.
