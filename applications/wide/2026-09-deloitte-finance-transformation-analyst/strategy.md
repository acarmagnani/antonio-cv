# Positioning: Deloitte, Analyst in Finance Transformation

**One line:** an analyst who has already produced the numbers a client committed capital against and built the tools that replaced the manual processes behind them, arriving at the CFO domain from the asset side rather than from an accounting degree.

**Profile chosen:** `profile.real-estate-investment`, kept from the block. It is the only profile that opens on feasibility and financial modelling and ends on capital and rigorous analysis, which is the vocabulary this programme reads for. The real estate framing stays because it is what he actually did, and because against a class of cand.merc graduates it is the part that is his own.

**Threads to lead with**

1. **Financial modelling with a decision at the end of it.** OSPA is the anchor: 15+ feasibility studies sizing investment and return per site, presented to developer clients. Felipe Hess adds the same exercise inside a practice, and Sweco's pricing tool parametrised fee bands and project economics so bids rested on figures. This is the finance craftsmanship the bootcamp is meant to build, started already.
2. **Tools that replaced manual work, and were adopted.** The Excel and VBA financial analysis tool that ingests monthly statements and PDF exports, categorises transactions and renders a decision dashboard, is the single most CFO-relevant thing in his record. Itaú's VBA inventory dashboard replaced a manual process at a bank and sped up procurement. The Grasshopper plugin at Isay Weinfeld cut a task from days to twenty minutes, and mattered mostly because colleagues actually took it up, which is the change-adoption half of any transformation project. Ramboll adds the automated risk assessment system consolidating client data into a live dashboard.

**Answering the form's questions:** there is no cover letter here, the form asks four required questions of 1440 characters each instead. The answers are written out in `form_answers.md`, ready to paste. `cover_letter.md` is not for upload; it was the source material for those answers and stays in the folder as the record of the positioning.

**The honest gap:** no accounting, no controlling or month-end closing, no ERP, and a master's in Architecture Computation rather than in business or economics. The claim is analytical craft, tooling and client delivery, not finance-function experience.
