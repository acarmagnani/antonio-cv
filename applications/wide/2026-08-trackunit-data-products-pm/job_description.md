# Product Manager — Data Products (Analytics Suite & Benchmarking) — Trackunit

Come make a difference in a fast-growing, innovative, Software Product Management team that combines a scale-up atmosphere with a global industry-leading product portfolio. You'll be part of building the future of Trackunit's connected ecosystem - helping shape products that connect people, data, and machines to build a smarter construction industry.

At Trackunit, we put our customers first. As a Product Manager, you'll work across teams and departments to guide software products from research and idea through to launch - connecting the customer, technical, and business worlds. You'll break down complex problems into clear, actionable steps and drive product development with focus, purpose, and measurable outcomes.

This role drives Trackunit's Data Products, including the Analytics Suite and Benchmarking product. These tools sit on top of Trackunit's connected data platform and give fleet operators, operations teams, and data-driven decision-makers a way to turn machine and site data into actionable insight. Data Products are one of Trackunit's highest-leverage growth areas: the ability to surface meaningful, industry-specific analysis is what keeps customers on the platform and drives expansion.

You will join the Platform Pod. The pod's mandate is to evolve IrisX into a Platform-as-a-Service: enabling AI enabled and faster development across the ecosystem, powering data-driven value creation, and unlocking multi-sided business models at construction scale.

You will be connected to one of our offices in Europe or North America with the possibility to work remotely in a flexible capacity.

## Who are you ideally?

- You've done product management in a B2B SaaS or platform/industrial software environment.
- You've built products for data-intensive user personas. You know what "good" looks like for a data analyst, BI team, or data scientist, and you understand the gap between what a data platform can technically do and what users actually adopt.
- You're a technical PM with genuine data stack depth (i.e. databricks), close enough to engineering to challenge scope and catch tradeoffs.
- You think and build AI-first. You reach for AI capabilities as a default design tool, not a bolt-on, and you know how to evaluate when AI genuinely reduces friction versus when it adds noise. You've shipped or closely partnered on AI-powered product features.
- You bring great structuring and analytical skills with the ability to implement strategic product plans and to proactively make things happen with a hands-on attitude.
- You're an effective communicator who knows how to work asynchronously and across time zones.
- You're experienced in the full product lifecycle: from backlog refinement and sprint execution to coordinating with go-to-market teams.
- You're adept at navigating trade-offs, bringing cross-functional clarity, and moving work forward.
- You bring commercial awareness and know how to define and measure progress with data and feedback loops.
- You focus on delivering impact, not just output, and you own what you build.

Don't meet every single requirement? No worries - if you're super excited about this role but your past experience doesn't align perfectly with every qualification, we encourage you to apply.

## Primary Responsibilities

- Own the Data Products roadmap: Set the strategy and priorities for the Analytics Suite, Benchmarking, and the data-facing experiences built on Trackunit's connected platform. Decide what gets built, deferred, or cut, and explain the reasoning to engineering, go-to-market teams, and leadership.
- Drive end-to-end product execution: Own the build process from backlog refinement to release, working closely with Engineering, Design, Product Marketing and other Go-To-Market teams.
- Lead go-to-market efforts: Coordinate with Marketing, Sales, and Customer Success.
- Create clarity across functions: Align teams on scope, trade-offs, and timelines.
- Partner with Data Engineering on platform surface: Co-define what Trackunit's data platform exposes through APIs, dashboards, and developer tools.
- Drive data product adoption: Work closely with Customer Success and key accounts to understand where data tools create real value, and close that loop back into discovery and prioritization.
