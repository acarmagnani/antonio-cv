## You keep our digital workplace secure, connected, and running smoothly

We are looking for a Digital Workplace Specialist to take hands-on ownership of the tools, devices, and security controls that our colleagues rely on every day. You will work across our full Microsoft 365 estate, from intranet and collaboration platforms to endpoint security, and take accountability for keeping it secure, well-governed, and easy to use, wherever in the world our colleagues are based.

You will, for example:

- **Global device deployment:** own and drive the global rollout and lifecycle management of our laptop estate, coordinating imaging, provisioning, shipping, and support across our regional offices
- **Security and Access:** take accountability for the security part of our setup, including day-to-day operation, monitoring, and tuning of Microsoft Defender and Microsoft Sentinel, as well as managing IAM setups, data privacy rules, and safe file-sharing practices
- **On-premises transition:** support and help drive our ongoing transition away from remaining on-premises solutions toward modern, cloud-first Microsoft 365 services
- **Tool Management:** set up and maintain platforms such as our Microsoft 365 company intranet, Teams, and SharePoint, ensuring they remain secure, current, and easy to use
- **User Support:** troubleshoot software and device issues and help staff across the organisation use our digital tools efficiently
- **Training and Adoption:** create clear guides and lead training sessions that help employees learn and adopt new technology with confidence
- **Process Improvement:** identify, test, and roll out new digital solutions that save time, strengthen security, and boost teamwork
- **Project delivery:** plan and launch software and hardware rollouts on time, tracking user adoption and success
- **Collaboration:** work closely with colleagues in IT Security, Data, and Business Applications to align on architecture, standards, and best practice

## You bring hands-on technical skill and a service-minded approach

With your background in digital workplace or IT support, you understand how to balance user needs with strong security practice, and you enjoy being the person people turn to when technology needs to just work.

We imagine that you:

- **Technical Knowledge:** have a strong understanding of the Microsoft 365 ecosystem (Teams, SharePoint, Intune, Entra ID), cloud collaboration tools, and solid IT troubleshooting skills
- **Security experience:** have practical experience with, or a genuine aptitude for, security tools such as Microsoft Defender and Microsoft Sentinel
- **Communication:** write and speak clearly, and can explain technical ideas to non-technical staff across cultures and time zones

- Are familiar with IAM, data privacy regulations (e.g. GDPR), and secure file-sharing practices
- Have supported a migration away from on-premises infrastructure to the cloud
- Have experience working across multiple countries, cultures, or time zones

## About Impact Fund Denmark

Impact Fund Denmark (IFU, the Investment Fund for Developing Countries) is a Danish impact investor contributing to green, just and inclusive societies as well as supporting the Sustainable Development Goals. Impact Fund Denmark provides risk capital to companies operating in developing countries across Africa, Asia, Latin America and parts of Europe. Investments are made on commercial terms in the form of equity, loans and guarantees. Impact Fund Denmark has co-invested in over 1,300 companies in more than 100 developing countries and emerging markets. Contracted investments total EUR 31 billion, of which Impact Fund Denmark has contributed EUR 3.4 billion. Capital under management will be growing from EUR 2.1 billion to EUR 5.0 billion over the next few years.
