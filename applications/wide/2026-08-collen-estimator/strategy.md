# Strategy, Collen Estimator (Copenhagen, preconstruction)

**Bucket:** `wide`. Ele não é orçamentista, não tem BoQ nem prelims, e o anúncio pede 3-5 anos em função semelhante mais diploma em quantity surveying. Aplica na mesma, sabendo disso.

**Positioning:** someone who has run tenders from the other side of the table and has priced work for a living, arriving from design and development rather than from quantity surveying.

**Profile used:** `profile.real-estate`, the feasibility, cost and Revit variant, because a contractor reads buildings and numbers before it reads investment vocabulary.

**Threads to lead with:**

- **Tendering is real experience, not an interest.** At Sweco he assembled bid teams for competitive international tenders, sourcing specialist consultants to evidence local and technical expertise, and wrote the technical and methodology sections of the proposals. He also built the pricing tool that parametrises expert fee bands and project economics behind bid decisions. That is bid management and pricing, on consultancy fees rather than construction packages.
- **He has read technical documents as a gate, not as reference.** Engineering design review at Sweco under FIDIC Yellow Book arrangements, checking specifications against subconsultants' drawings, and at Itaú-Unibanco reviewing documentation from architecture and engineering firms against NBR standards before construction could be authorised.
- **Development cost, tested against a programme.** At Felipe Hess he ran financial feasibility testing saleable area, unit mix and development cost against the buildable programme; at OSPA he sized investment and return across 15+ studies.
- **Buildability and digital tools.** Coordinating structural, HVAC, electrical and lighting into a single Revit model at Isay Weinfeld, BIM documentation and construction drawings at Felipe Hess, and the supply chain mapping of 20+ critical raw materials at Ramboll for the procurement and benchmarking duties.

**Honest gaps:** no bills of quantities, no prelim allowances, no subcontractor quote comparison, no cost planning software, no Danish construction rates, and no QS degree. The letter names this once, briefly, and then argues from the tender and cost work he does have.

**Language:** letter in English.
