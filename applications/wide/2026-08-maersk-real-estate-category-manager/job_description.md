# Real Estate Category Manager — Maersk Corporate Real Estate (Copenhagen)

Full-time, office-based (5 days/week). Maersk Corporate Real Estate, part of Corporate Procurement & Strategic Projects (Asset Strategy & Strategic Partnerships). Reports to the Head of Corporate Real Estate.

Maersk Corporate Real Estate holds global responsibility for the Maersk Real Estate Category and the strategic direction of the office portfolio (400+ corporate offices worldwide, 100,000+ colleagues). The Real Estate Category Manager takes proactive ownership of the Real Estate category for a designated region, leading the full real estate lifecycle: office portfolio strategies, lease transactions, M&A integrations, and workplace solutions. Acts as a trusted advisor to senior business leaders. Objective: a compliant, cost-efficient, professionally managed real estate portfolio.

## Key Responsibilities

**Portfolio Strategy & Workplace Planning**
- Lead development and execution of global and regional office portfolio strategies.
- Shape workplace solutions supporting business growth, employee experience, operational efficiency.
- Provide strategic guidance on consolidation opportunities and long-term footprint design.

**Transactions & Lease Management**
- Lead end-to-end lease transactions (market analysis, negotiation strategy, execution).
- Manage renewals, exits, expansions, and new site acquisitions across the region.
- Proactively manage critical dates, risks, and opportunities.

**M&A Real Estate Integration**
- Assess real estate impacts, integrate new offices, align with Maersk's workplace standards.
- Provide recommendations on consolidation, divestment, or expansion following acquisitions.

**Stakeholder Engagement**
- Engage senior stakeholders across regions and functions as a trusted advisor on real estate strategy.
- Collaborate with HR, Finance, Technology, and business leaders.
- Communicate portfolio insights, risks, and opportunities clearly and proactively.

**Tools, Governance & Insights**
- Own and improve key tools and frameworks: business cases, Heads of Terms, Charters, TCO & TVO models, investment proposals, playbooks, milestone frameworks.
- Maintain a data-driven portfolio and provide insights supporting decision-making.

**Portfolio Oversight**
- Keep the global real estate portfolio updated, accurate, proactively managed.
- Monitor performance, utilization, and cost efficiency across the regional footprint.
- Drive continuous improvement and strategic optimization of workplaces.

## Who we're looking for

- 3-5 years of real estate experience, preferably global, involving some leadership exposure. Multinational corporate or consultancy experience especially noted.
- Relevant education; a flair for people; personal presence to engage credibly with senior stakeholders.
- Comfortable at the intersection of business strategy and real estate expertise; translates business needs into sound, data-driven recommendations; pushes back when the data disagrees.
- Commercial acumen and analytical rigor. Challenges the status quo constructively, takes ownership, an eye for where the category can grow.
- Fluent English (prerequisite). No Danish required.
