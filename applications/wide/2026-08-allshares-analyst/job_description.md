Allshares is a fast-growing SaaS company and a leading expert in equity and incentive design and management. Our team of 250+ specialists are located in the US, Finland, Sweden, Portugal, France and Switzerland. We're dedicated to tailoring the desired incentive models for our clients as well as making compensation strategies simpler and more effective for companies of all sizes.
