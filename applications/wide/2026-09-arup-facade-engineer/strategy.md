# Arup, Facade Engineer (Copenhagen), Technical Specialist Services

## Positioning

An architect who has been doing facade work all along, inside the project rather than as a separate specialism, applying to do it as the specialism. Not a data person applying to a technical seat, and not a property person: this CV reads as someone who resolves how a building is actually made, and who happens to bring computation and material research with him.

## The two threads to lead with

1. **Facade and envelope work in practice.** At Isay Weinfeld he moved between the design team and the technical development team on large residential, hotel and mixed-use buildings, which meant carrying facades from the compositional stage into resolved build-ups: materials, fixings and jointing, preliminary structural sizing, and reviewing the structural and services engineers' input against the design intent. Felipe Hess is the same work at smaller scale, from preliminary study to construction documentation. This is the thread the whole application rests on, and it was not in `content_base.yaml` before 2026-09-07.

2. **Novel materials and computational method.** Blue biomass as a rammed composite with LCA metrics and parametric simulation, which became the material basis for the Royal Danish Academy's seven-metre installation at the Venice Biennale 2025 with CITA, GXN and 3XN; origami-based deployable structures at Southeast University; the Grasshopper plugin in Python at Isay Weinfeld. Arup's Copenhagen team is materials, facades and fire in one group, so the material research is not a side interest here, it is the thing that distinguishes him from other architects applying.

## Profile

`profile.proptech`, the block default. It is the only profile that opens on computational design and parametric work rather than on finance or human rights. Its weakness is real and worth knowing: it says "real estate and cities" and "spatial and financial data", which is a half-step away from a facade team. If this family of jobs recurs, a profile variant written for technical architecture is the first thing to author.

## The honest gap

He has never held the facade consultant's seat, which is a different job from the architect's: advising another architect, specifying systems, running the package commercially. No structural glass or cladding specification to consultancy standard, no curtain-wall system procurement, no quantified building physics (U-values, condensation risk, thermal bridging), no Society of Facade Engineers, no Danish. The posting does not require most of that, since it offers training and senior support and lists an architecture degree first, but it is where an interview will push.

## What this is not

Not a step toward real estate investment, which is why the application sits in `wide/`. It is a strong seat at a great firm in the city he lives in, and it would make him a specialist rather than an analyst. Worth being clear-eyed that those are different careers.
