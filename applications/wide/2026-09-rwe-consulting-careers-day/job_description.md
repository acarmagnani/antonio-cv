## About RWE

RWE stands for transformation, innovation and sustainability. As one of the world's leading producers of electricity from renewable energy sources, we want to be carbon neutral by 2040 and enable people around the world to live sustainably. To achieve this, we are looking for dynamic and motivated people who want to join us in shaping a sustainable and secure energy future.

RWE Consulting is the inhouse-consultancy of the RWE Group. Our consultants work hand in hand with RWE's Corporate Strategy and Sustainability teams to implement the Group's key strategic initiatives. We are always looking for new colleagues to join us on our journey towards the energy transition. Be part of our vision and take the first step to an exciting future!

We value diversity and therefore welcome all applications, irrespective of sex, disability, nationality, ethnic and social background, religion and beliefs, age or sexual orientation and identity.
