# Positioning: RWE Consulting Careers Day

**One line:** a recent master's graduate who has already done sustainability and climate work inside two engineering consultancies, on energy and water infrastructure that had to satisfy lenders, rather than someone who is interested in the energy transition in principle.

**Profile chosen:** `profile.esg-consulting`, from the block. It opens on sustainability consulting and the built environment, names the climate risk work on internationally financed infrastructure, and ends on sustainability and rigorous analysis, which is what the event screens for.

**Threads to lead with**

1. **Energy and infrastructure that had money behind it.** Sweco is the anchor: water, energy and transport projects financed by the World Bank, EBRD, KfW and EIB, with climate risk assessments written to JASPERS and EBRD climate-proofing guidance. That is the closest thing in his record to RWE's own world, and it carries the habit of evidencing a claim to a standard someone with capital at stake will review.
2. **Consulting delivery for corporate clients.** Ramboll supplies the due diligence, the automated risk assessment system and the critical raw materials market study, which shows he has worked inside a management consultancy and delivered to corporate clients rather than only studying the subject.

**What the portal asks for:** a CV and contact details only, so the invitation decision is made on the CV alone. A cover letter was written anyway, at Antonio's request, for a portal message box, for the reply if an invitation arrives, and for the recruiter conversation on the day.

**Selection note:** the ESG block is used with no delta. The event asks for nothing the block leaves out.

**The honest gap:** no power markets, no electricity generation, and no strategy-consulting case experience, which is what the day itself will test. If the invitation comes, the case study is where the preparation has to go.
