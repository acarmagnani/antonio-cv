# Project Planner, Project Management Consultancy — Laminar Projects

Location: UK (London office), hybrid with site travel across Europe and the Middle East. Company of 220+, founded 2017. High-end project management consultancy and construction tech developer (product: Shape).

## Purpose of the role

Enhance the delivery of complex projects through project planning to the highest standards. Devise strategies and tactics to improve how the project is delivered, translate that knowledge into a logic-linked plan, analyse it using planning software, and use the plan to help teams understand what to prioritise.

## The team

Part of the Project Management Consultancy team, which focuses on understanding the real problems of projects, implementing the best solutions to those problems, and developing scalable best practices, tools and technology. Specifically the Project Planning Team, led by a planning discipline director.

## Top three objectives

- Make construction projects deliver to near perfection.
- Create scalable and repeatable solutions to common problems.
- Develop yourself and everyone around you.

## Top five responsibilities

- Own the plan: own the project plan for one or more projects and ensure the project is working to it effectively, with delays mitigated and managed commercially.
- Own the outcome: responsible for the project team's understanding and focus on the plan.
- Report and inform: provide simple, actionable reports to stakeholders at all levels.
- Make scalable solutions: develop scalable and repeatable best practices and tools with the team.
- Develop everyone: maximise the development of the team and educate clients to improve as well.

## Must have

- Construction engineering background: 2+ years of experience on construction projects, experience in project planning (both software and principles), decent Excel skills, and a strong interest in data.
- Communication skills: proficient written and verbal English, with the charisma and influence to get construction people to do new things and take the best approach.
- Leadership capabilities: leads self proactively and leads others by asking the right questions and challenging them.
- Impact: a track record of making projects and organisations better beyond the basics, implementing scalable, repeatable solutions with significant positive impact.

## Projects and clients

Data centres (100+ delivered internationally, many fast-tracked and multi-package), infrastructure (defence, rail, highways, including HS2), high tech (advanced manufacturing, pharmaceuticals, semiconductors), complex buildings, high-containment labs and R&D campuses, $30bn+ in power generation and transmission. Clients are major owners/operators and main contractors, project values £1m to £1bn+.
