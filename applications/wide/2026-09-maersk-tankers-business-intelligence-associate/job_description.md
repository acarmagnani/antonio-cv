# Business Intelligence Associate — Maersk Tankers

**Company:** Maersk Tankers (A.P. Moller Holding)
**Location:** Copenhagen, Denmark
**Team:** Data Insights (six people)
**Contact in posting:** Daniela Gaberova, Daniela.Gaberova@maersktankers.com

## Your opportunity to join the adventure

In Data Insights, you will join a team of six talented and welcoming colleagues. You will be responsible for translating business requirements into reporting, analytics, and data solutions, while designing, developing, and maintaining Power BI dashboards, reports, and data models.

This role supports the development and maintenance of data pipelines and ETL processes, ensuring data quality, consistency, and reliability across reporting solutions.

It involves collaborating with stakeholders to identify opportunities for process improvements and data-driven decision-making, supporting financial and operational reporting needs across the organization, contributing to the ongoing development and execution of the Data Insights strategy, and participating in the automation and optimization of business processes.

## Are you part of the solution?

At Maersk Tankers, you can come as you are and be among people who will expand your horizon. As our new Business Intelligence Associate, we do, however, expect that you can contribute with the following:

- A relevant degree in Data Analytics, Computer Science, Information Systems, Engineering, or a related field
- 1-3 years of experience working with Business Intelligence, reporting, and analytics solutions
- Knowledge of SQL and experience writing queries to extract and analyze data
- Understanding of relational databases and database concepts
- Understanding of data modeling principles and ETL processes
- Experience developing reports and dashboards in Power BI
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with a structured approach to work
- Excellent communication skills and the ability to work closely with both technical and business stakeholders
- Good English communication skills, both verbal and written

## About Maersk Tankers

Maersk Tankers and affiliated companies operate one of the largest tanker fleets in the world through direct ownership and commercial management solutions for shipowners. Our mission is to be experts in owning and operating tankers and gas carriers through the energy transition, building on a legacy that dates to 1928. Maersk Tankers employs approximately 300 people in Denmark, Singapore, India, UK and the U.S. and is domiciled in Copenhagen, Denmark. Maersk Tankers is part of A.P. Moller Group owned by A.P. Moller Holding.
