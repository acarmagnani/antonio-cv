Joining Tetra Tech as a graduate is more than starting a new job, you'll become part of the Next Gen Talent development programme, designed to support you as you build your career. Alongside hands-on experience and exposure to real projects, you'll benefit from a structured programme of regular learning, training and development, supported throughout by experienced colleagues.

## Purpose and scope of the role

Join Tetra Tech's Sustainable Building Design team and help inform the design and construction of environmentally conscious buildings and masterplans. You'll work with architects, engineers, clients and wider stakeholders on energy assessments, embodied carbon studies, circular economy strategies and sustainability frameworks including BREEAM, WELL and DREAM.

## Your impact

You'll support clients to achieve their sustainability goals by contributing to energy modelling, life cycle assessment, material and technology research, renewable energy opportunities and sustainability reporting. Your input will help improve building performance, reduce carbon and strengthen design decisions from concept through delivery.

## Five core capabilities

- **Sustainable Design Integration** — work collaboratively to embed sustainable principles into project designs and support environmentally responsive outcomes.
- **Energy and Carbon Analysis** — use energy modelling and life cycle assessment to improve energy performance and reduce embodied carbon.
- **Certification Support** — assist assessors with BREEAM, WELL and DREAM certification work.
- **Innovation and Resource Efficiency** — identify low-carbon materials, renewable energy opportunities and circular economy strategies.
- **Reporting and Stakeholder Communication** — prepare reports, planning submissions and presentations, while advising clients and multidisciplinary teams clearly and professionally.

## Training, qualifications and experience

A minimum 2:1 degree (or equivalent) in architecture, architectural engineering, building services, renewable energy, sustainable design or a related discipline (MEng or MSc) is required. Proficiency in Microsoft Office is essential; AutoCAD, Revit, Adobe Creative Cloud and energy modelling tools such as IES, Tas, EnergyPlus or DesignBuilder are desirable. Strong communication, teamwork, sustainability awareness and suitability for security clearance are required.
