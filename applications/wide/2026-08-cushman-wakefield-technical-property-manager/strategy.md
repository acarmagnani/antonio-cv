# Strategy, Cushman & Wakefield, Technical Property Manager, Asset Services, España

**Positioning:** someone who has already been the technical filter on a real estate portfolio from the owner's side, checking buildings against norms before work was authorised, tracking their energy and certifications, and reviewing what contractors actually delivered, and who now wants to do that on managed assets for third-party clients.

**Profile chosen:** `profile.real-estate-esg-data`. It is the one that puts real estate and sustainability in the same sentence and names building energy and environmental performance analysis, which is the ESG half of this mandate. The investment-flavoured profiles would promise an analyst the team is not hiring.

**Bucket:** `wide`. He clears every stated requirement, so the odds are the best of any real estate posting he has sent, but this is an operational seat rather than the analyst or advisory direction he is aiming at. It should not be counted as a target role in the gap analysis.

**CV note:** written by hand, no preset. None of the three blocks covers technical management of buildings in operation, so the selection was built for it: compliance and technical review lead, contractors and tenders follow, energy and ESG carry the differentiator, and the cost-tracking tools close it. Feasibility, zoning depth, the house price model, the human rights strand and all parametric work are out.

**Threads to lead with:**

- **The Itaú year is almost a job description match.** Inside a bank's own real estate function he reviewed technical documentation from architecture and engineering firms against building, accessibility and fire-safety norms as the gate before construction was authorised (cumplimiento técnico-legal, and the same discipline as controlling tenant fit-out works), assessed the environmental and energy performance of buildings the bank was acquiring or leasing through consumption data, efficiency ratings and certifications (utilities, consumos, certificaciones), supported the technical review of new office towers under construction, and built a VBA dashboard tracking corporate inventory across locations that sped up procurement for fit-outs.
- **Supplier control from the consultancy side.** At Sweco he reviews engineering designs under FIDIC Yellow Book contracts against subconsultants' drawings, assembles specialist teams for international tenders, and built a pricing tool parametrizing fee bands and project economics. That covers licitaciones, gestión del contrato and the budget half of the role.
- **The ESG layer is real and it is building-level.** At Ramboll he developed the firm's real estate ESG due diligence service, centred on building energy and decarbonisation under EU building performance regulation, and produced a supplier-facing resource for real estate clients aimed at contractors and subcontractors on site.
- **Gaps to leave unclaimed:** no property management track record, no capex plan or invoice approval ownership, no gastos comunes, no tenant relations, and no familiarity with the Spanish technical-legal inspection regime (ITE, OCA, RITE). The letter does not go near any of them.
