# Architect — BYREDO, Stockholm

**Company:** BYREDO (luxury fragrance and lifestyle brand)
**Location:** Stockholm, Sweden
**Contract:** full-time, permanent, preferred start as soon as possible
**Reports to:** Head of Architecture
**Application requires:** resume, personal letter and portfolio, all in English

---

## The opportunity

BYREDO is looking for an Architect to join the team in Stockholm. Reporting to the Head of Architecture, the role contributes to design projects within a creative and dynamic environment.

## What you'll get to do

As an Architect at BYREDO, you will design and visualize spaces three-dimensionally based on creative briefs. The work involves visualizing buildings, interiors, furniture and installations, and creating technical and production drawings. Beyond executing designs, you will provide insight within your field, contributing ideas and solutions. You will shape and develop concepts for standalone stores, shop-in-shops, travel retail setups, offices, furniture, display tools and other objects.

You will collaborate with the Head of Architecture and the Architect Team, alongside Project Managers in the Store Design Team and Visual Merchandisers in the Visual Merchandising Team, and must be open to travel when required.

- Develop designs and visualizations based on creative briefs.
- Collaborate with the Head of Architecture to create innovative concepts.
- Produce three-dimensional visualizations of physical spaces, interiors or objects.
- Prepare presentations and design layouts for key stakeholders.
- Prepare detailed technical drawings.
- Create production drawings for execution.
- Work with the store design team to develop fixtures and finishes for various projects.
- Explore new creative ideas to evolve and enhance the existing store design concept.
- Support site verifications and visits to ensure design quality.

## We'd love to meet you if you have

BYREDO believes in the power of creativity and craftsmanship to create timeless products that are meaningful and inspirational, approaching the work with openness to learn from each other and embracing individuality and diversity.

You thrive in a creative and dynamic setting where attention to detail and aesthetic precision are key, with a strong ability to translate ideas into tangible designs, approaching each project with both artistic sensibility and technical expertise.

- Several years of experience within interiors and/or architecture.
- An aesthetic sense that aligns with the brand's vision.
- A strong interest in retail design and creating impactful spaces.
- Skill at interpreting a brief and developing creative ideas and solutions.
- Adaptable, thriving in a dynamic environment.
- A strong eye for detail and quality.
- Comfortable in a fast-paced setting, managing multiple priorities.
- Willingness to travel as needed.

## What they offer

An entrepreneurial, creative and welcoming work culture, learning and development opportunities, an international company with room to grow, and a competitive compensation and benefits package.
