What buys it is that the fit is near total, the chance is the highest in the pipeline, Stockholm is priority-one geography and the process runs in English.

**One line:** an architect from a high-end studio who carries a space from a brief through to production drawings, and who has both the visualisation craft and the material research behind it.

**Threads to lead with:**

1. **Isay Weinfeld, and specifically the envelope work.** Sitting across the design team and the technical development team, taking facades from the compositional stage through materials, fixings, jointing and preliminary structural sizing into documentation. Translated to this job, that is a store concept carried into fixtures, finishes and production drawings, which is the posting almost line by line. The studio itself is the aesthetic credential a brand like this screens for.
2. **The visualisation practice he founded and ran.** An architectural visualization studio with 10+ clients, producing renders, humanised plans and visualisations to communicate a design. "Produce three-dimensional visualizations of physical spaces, interiors or objects" is the first responsibility listed, and this is direct evidence rather than an adjacent claim.

**Third thread, and the one that separates him from other architects:** the master's material work. Blue biomass, seashells and algae in a rammed composite with LCA metrics run alongside parametric simulation, which became the material basis for the school's installation at the Venice Architecture Biennale 2025, where he produced the physical panels in the seven-metre structure at the Arsenale with CITA, GXN and 3XN. Furniture, displays, installations and objects are half of this role, and a built Biennale piece is the strongest thing he has for it.

- **No retail design experience.** No stores, no shop-in-shops, no travel retail, no visual merchandising. The posting asks for "a strong interest in retail design", not experience in it, so this is answerable, but it is the honest gap.
