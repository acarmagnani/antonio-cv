Dear Hiring Team at Rystad Energy,

I trained as an architect in Brazil and kept drifting toward the questions that came before the design. Why here, what the data actually says, and what would make the decision better. That pull took me out of practice and eventually from São Paulo to Copenhagen for an M.Arch. in Architecture Computation at The Royal Danish Academy, a programme about using data and modelling to make better decisions on the built environment. What held my attention over the past two years turned out to be energy and infrastructure rather than buildings, which is why an advisory team built on objective analysis of the energy transition is where I want to go next.

At Sweco I supported consulting teams on water, energy and transport projects across emerging economies, financed by the World Bank, EBRD, KfW and EIB. I authored climate risk assessments under JASPERS and EBRD climate-proofing guidance, screening hazards against projections data and rating each asset by exposure and vulnerability. On a field mission across Baku's water supply system I assessed asset condition and gathered data through inspections and interviews with national water agencies, then built the geospatial databases the analysis ran on. I also wrote methodology sections for competitive international tenders and built a pricing tool parametrizing expert fee bands and project economics.

Before that, at Ramboll Management Consulting, I led an internal market study mapping the supply chains of more than twenty critical raw materials under the EU Critical Raw Materials Act, connecting regulatory drivers to concrete business development targets, and ran a peer benchmarking analysis comparing a client against its competitors to identify gaps and positioning opportunities. I also integrated AI research tools into our due diligence workflows to source and cite risk data at high volume.

What connects all of it is quantitative work on assets and markets where the answer has to hold up rather than please whoever commissioned it. That is the part of Rystad's positioning I actually believe in, and Oslo is where I would want to do it.

All the best,
Antonio Carmagnani
