Dear Hiring Team at BCG,

I trained as an architect in Brazil and kept moving away from the drawing and toward the question that comes before it, which is where the decision actually sits. That took me from São Paulo to Copenhagen for an M.Arch. in Architecture Computation at The Royal Danish Academy, a quantitative programme built on data, modelling and code, and from there into consulting, first at Ramboll and then at Sweco. BCG says that a range of perspectives is what sparks change on a team. I would be arriving with the built environment, infrastructure and sustainability as a working knowledge rather than a reading list, and with the method already learned in two Copenhagen consultancies.

Ramboll Management Consulting is where I learned most of that method. I ran a peer benchmarking analysis for a corporate client, comparing its practices against industry competitors across several service areas and turning the gaps into positioning recommendations. I built a fully automated risk-assessment system in Excel that pulled scattered data from across a client's business units into one dashboard that updated as inputs changed. And I led an internal market study covering the supply chains of more than twenty critical raw materials, connecting incoming regulation to concrete business development targets for the practice.

At Sweco the work has been closer to the client's ground. I joined a field mission across Baku's water supply system, assessing asset condition and identifying technical, social and environmental risks through inspections and interviews, then sat with national water agencies and government counterparts to align on rehabilitation priorities. Learning a system that size from scratch, quickly, in a country I had never worked in, is the part of consulting I would happily do every month.

I am not arriving from a strategy house, and that is the one line in your list I cannot tick. What I bring instead is three years of turning open problems into structured, quantitative answers, and a domain most consultants have to learn from a deck. A firm that sells total transformation across sectors is where I want to test that.

All the best,
Antonio Carmagnani
