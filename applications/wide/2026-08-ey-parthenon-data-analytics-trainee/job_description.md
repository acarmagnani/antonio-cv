# Trainee, Data & Analytics — EY-Parthenon, Helsinki

**Company:** EY-Parthenon (EYP Data & Analytics, Nordic-wide team)
**Location:** Helsinki, FI
**Deadline:** 20 September 2026
**Start:** spring 2027 (flexible, agreed with the selected candidate)
**Contact:** rekrytointi@fi.ey.com

---

Are you passionate about understanding what drives business performance, the latest technology, and M&A? Are you excited about finding new and innovative ways to solve problems? With EYP Data & Analytics you will have the opportunity to be part of a new wave of business analytics within the world of M&A, helping our clients get to faster and deeper insights than ever before.

## The opportunity: Your next adventure awaits

In your role you will help our clients uncover the key drivers behind their businesses by building new solutions, analysing structured and unstructured datasets, and bridging the gap between data owners and users. Whether you are working with a private equity client looking to sell a portfolio company or guiding a corporate client through their first ever acquisition, no project is ever the same and every transaction presents its own unique set of challenges and analyses.

The one area that will be consistent throughout is the use of data driven solutions to analyse large and complex datasets, building sophisticated transaction driven analyses to help address our clients' challenges in fast-changing markets. The central driver of the Data & Analytics group is a constant ambition to improve the M&A process, recognizing the significant untapped value that sits in the ever-growing volume of data our world is generating.

You will be a part of a Nordic-wide team, a group with a diverse set of backgrounds including data science, engineering, finance, and accounting (amongst others). You will have the opportunity to work on projects both in Finland, as well as abroad, working side by side with talented and driven colleagues who will give you the support and guidance to succeed from day one.

## What We Look For

- Passion for learning and commanding the latest innovations and technology trends
- Analytical thinkers excited about solving complex problems in a fast-paced environment
- Dynamic team players who work well with people with different personal and technical backgrounds

## Qualifications

- You are studying towards a master's or equivalent degree in the field of accounting, finance, economics or engineering
- Strong academic performance
- General business acumen and interest in finance
- Excellent communication and presentation skills in English
- Experience in data processing using for example Python and data visualization tools, such as Power BI or Tableau

## Ideally, You'll Also Have

- Familiarity with technologies like Databricks, Snowflake, DataIku, AI/ML, cloud platforms (Azure, AWS, GCP), and Git (GitHub, GitLab, Azure DevOps)
- Finnish working proficiency considered as an advantage

## What's In It For You

At EY we put our colleagues at the centre, where you will have the ability to have a large impact on developing your career based on your own personal interests and goals, regardless of whether that includes industries and clients or developing your knowledge within specifics tools, technologies, and areas. On top of this you will receive opportunities to get involved in our exciting projects such as volunteer work and engagement with our campus ambassador team.

## What You Can Expect

- To apply, please submit your resume, cover letter and transcripts by September 20, 2026 on our career website.
- We review applications on an ongoing basis during the application period and may begin interviewing candidates before the application deadline.
- We are looking for a trainee to join us in spring 2027 in our Helsinki office. However, we are flexible regarding the exact start date, which will be agreed upon together with the selected candidate and the team.

Candidates selected for the position will be required to undergo a background check: submission of a study transcript or degree certificate, and verification of work permit in Finland.
