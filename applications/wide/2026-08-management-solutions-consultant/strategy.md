# Strategy — Management Solutions, incorporación de consultoría

**Positioning:** analytical raw material with consulting mileage already on it. Management Solutions hires engineers and quantitative graduates and trains them into risk, process and financial-information work, so the application does not compete on a finance degree it cannot produce. It competes on three things they actually test for: a quantitative master's, real risk-methodology work already done for corporate clients, and tools he builds rather than operates.

**Profile chosen:** `profile.real-estate-esg-data`. It is the only profile that names financial modeling, ESG due diligence and climate risk in the same breath as Excel, Power BI and Python, which maps onto their two heaviest service lines (gestión y control de riesgos, información de gestión y financiera) plus the "manejo avanzado de herramientas informáticas" requirement. `profile.esg-consulting` would narrow him to sustainability, which is only one of their practices and not the one this generic intake feeds.

**Threads to lead with:**

1. **The Colt salience methodology at Ramboll.** Scoring risks across business units and supply chains by probability, scale and scope to prioritize automatically, built as an automated Excel system with a dashboard that updated dynamically. This is the anchor. It is a risk-rating methodology delivered to a corporate client, which is the closest analogue in his history to what Management Solutions sells.
2. **Sweco, risk under a regulatory framework.** Climate risk assessments following JASPERS and EBRD climate-proofing guidance, rating risk by exposure and vulnerability asset by asset, plus a pricing tool parametrizing fee bands and project economics so bid decisions could be tested. Regulated methodology on one side, project economics on the other.
3. **OSPA, the numbers.** 15+ feasibility studies in Brazil and the US, sizing investment and return against buildable area, unit mix and price per square metre, presented to developer clients.

**The degree question:** the requisitos list does not include architecture. The letter names this once, directly, and reframes the M.Arch in Architecture Computation as the quantitative degree it actually is (data, modeling, machine learning). Trying to slip it past the filter unmentioned is worse than owning it in one sentence.

**Language:** letter in Spanish, CV in English, per `cover_letter_base.md`.
