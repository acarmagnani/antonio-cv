# Deloitte Denmark, Analyst / Consultant, HR Strategy & Technology

## The angle

He is not applying as an HR person, because he is not one and pretending otherwise fails at the first question. He applies through the two doors the posting leaves open on purpose: the degree line accepts "IT, or a related field", and the practice sits inside Technology & Transformation, whose subject is digital transformation. The pitch is therefore a management consultant with a computational toolkit, who has spent three years building the tools that client functions then run on, and who wants to apply that to how organisations work rather than to how buildings do.

## Why this is arguable rather than a stretch

Ramboll Management Consulting is the name of the firm on his CV, so "experience from the consulting industry" is answered directly, not by analogy. Inside it he did the things this posting lists as the job: workshop and training materials for a corporate client, peer benchmarking against industry competitors, an automated Excel risk system that consolidated data into one dashboard, AI tools integrated into a research workflow, and an internal market study whose stated purpose was to find prospective clients and new service lines. Change the subject matter from human rights to human capital and the verbs are unchanged.

The technology half is stronger than the average candidate for this seat will bring. Python, SQL, Power BI and Excel/VBA are real, and there are three separate instances of him building a tool that other people then used: the Grasshopper plugin adopted across 10+ projects at Isay Weinfeld, the VBA inventory dashboard that replaced a manual process at Itau, and the Ramboll salience system. Adoption is the hard part of any technology implementation, and he has actually done it.

## The real gaps, stated plainly

No HR content of any kind: no HR life-cycle processes, no HR operating model work, no HCM platform (Workday, SuccessFactors, Dayforce), which is what "HR technology" means inside this practice. The M.Arch is a computation degree, but it is a computation degree in architecture, and a screener reading fast may not extend it to "IT or a related field". The practice describes itself as the advisory side of HR transformation, which means the seat is strategy and operating model work, not engineering, so his tool-building is evidence of aptitude rather than evidence of having done the job.

The one genuinely HR-shaped line he owns is the Bangladesh branch office setup at Sweco, where he drafted employment contracts and ran the insurance onboarding. It is small, and it is real, and it is in the CV for that reason.

## What the CV does

No preset. Built by hand, because all three blocks are built to point at the built environment and that is the least relevant thing about him here. `profile.proptech` leads, because it is the only profile variant that puts technology and data at the centre, which is the argument being made. Consulting craft carries the body: Ramboll keeps six bullets and is the spine, Sweco keeps five, and the two architecture practices are cut to the tool story and the anchor respectively. Every real estate feasibility and zoning bullet is out.

Known compromise, worth saying out loud: `profile.proptech` is scoped to "real estate and cities", which is narrower than this job. There is no management-consulting-plus-digital profile variant in `content_base.yaml`, and writing one is authoring work to do with Antonio, not something to invent during tailoring. The two 200-word answers carry the positioning that the profile cannot.

## The application itself

There is no cover letter. Deloitte asks for two answers of at most 200 words each, plus resume and bachelor's and master's transcripts. The answers live in `application_questions.md`. Both are free prose written outside the `letter_base.yaml` pipeline, because that pipeline builds letters and this is not one, so they are the one part of this application that has not been machine-validated and need Antonio's own read.

Bucket: `wide/`. Not because the application is weak, but because `target/` feeds the skills-gap analysis, and counting "HR life-cycle processes" and "Workday" as requirements he should close would send that file in a direction he has not chosen.
