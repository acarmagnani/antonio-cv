# Deloitte, HR Strategy & Technology, application questions

Deloitte replaces the cover letter with these two answers. The portal caps each at **1440 characters**, not 200 words as the posting text says. Also required in the portal: resume, bachelor's transcript, master's transcript.

These are free prose, written outside the `letter_base.yaml` pipeline, because that pipeline renders cover letters and these are not one. They follow the same voice rules and every claim traces to `content_base.yaml`, but they have not been machine-validated. Read them before sending.

## 1. What interests and motivates you about pursuing a career in HR Strategy & Technology consulting, and what unique contributions do you envision bringing to this role?

What draws me to HR Strategy and Technology is that it sits where decisions about how an organisation works meet the systems that carry them out, and that intersection has been the constant in my work. At Ramboll Management Consulting I built an automated risk assessment system that consolidated data across a client's business units and supply chains into a single dashboard, where the analysis was never the difficult part, because the difficult part was designing something people would still use after the consultants left.

I learned that most directly at Isay Weinfeld Architects, where I wrote a tool that cut a routine task from several days to about twenty minutes, and building it was the smaller half of the work. The rest was sitting with the colleagues who had been doing it by hand to establish what they needed, presenting it in a studio workshop, and supporting its adoption across more than ten projects, which taught me that a tool nobody trusts enough to use has not been finished. That is why a people-centred approach to transformation reads to me as a practical requirement rather than a soft one.

What I would bring is consulting delivery together with the ability to build the thing myself, working in Python, SQL, Power BI and Excel with VBA, alongside the human half of the same work, creating workshop and training materials for a corporate client and benchmarking its practices against competitors.

## 2. How have your educational and/or professional experiences prepared you to work as an Analyst at Deloitte?

My most direct preparation is Ramboll Management Consulting, where I conducted due diligence for corporate clients against the UN Guiding Principles and OECD Guidelines, produced workshop and training materials, ran a peer benchmarking analysis against a client's industry competitors, and led an internal market study that connected regulatory drivers to concrete business development targets. That is most of what this role describes, with human rights as the subject matter rather than human capital.

At Sweco I supported advisory teams on infrastructure projects financed by the World Bank, EBRD, KfW and EIB, working in multidisciplinary international teams to the standards those lenders require. I engaged national agencies and government counterparts during field missions to align on priorities and collect project data, contributed the methodology sections of competitive tenders, and supported the setup of a new branch office in Bangladesh, where I drafted the employment contracts and managed the insurance onboarding.

Underneath both is an M.Arch in Architecture Computation at The Royal Danish Academy, a programme built around using data to improve how decisions get made, where I built a predictive model that finished in the top two per cent of its competition. I have run my own consulting practice since 2018, responsible for client acquisition, scoping, pricing and delivery across more than ten clients.
