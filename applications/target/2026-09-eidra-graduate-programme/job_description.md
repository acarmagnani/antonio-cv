# Graduate Programme, Eidra, Nordic consultancy collective

**Company:** Eidra (partner-owned Nordic consultancy collective, ~1400 people, 8 offices; formed in 2019 when Curamando merged with Kurppa Hosk, Animal, Conversionista and Keybroker)
**Location:** home base at Eidra House (Stockholm), rotation across the collective's companies
**Format:** 12-month graduate programme with company rotation, then a permanent role in one of the Eidra companies

---

The Eidra Graduate Programme is your entry point to a Nordic consultancy collective with global clients, bold ideas and real impact. As a graduate, you'll test your capabilities across our leading companies ranging from creative studios to tech-driven strategy firms. You will get hands-on experience, tailored training, and a community that spans disciplines, cities and ways of thinking. This isn't about choosing career path. It is about exploring, growing, and test where your interest and contribution aligns best.

## What we look for

We're looking for ambitious and versatile graduates ready to shape the future of consulting.

It's less about what you studied, and more about how you approach problems. You might be:

- An engineer who thinks like a strategist
- An economist who loves solving real-world problems
- A psychologist who understands behaviour and systems
- A marketer who's obsessed with analytics
- A designer who thrives at the intersection of design and business
- Or something entirely different, we're open to all kinds of thinkers and doers

You should be:

- **Curious:** eager to explore new industries, methods, and ideas
- **Creative:** able to find new angles to tough problems
- **Structured:** capable of managing projects and deadlines
- **Collaborative:** a strong communicator who thrives in teams
- **Adaptable:** ready to learn fast and embrace change

Bonus if you have:

- Internship or work experience
- Background in interdisciplinary or analytical fields
- A master's degree (in any field), or relevant experience that compensates for one

## What you'll get

12-month programme with company rotation. International onboarding and a tight-knit graduate cohort. Permanent role in one of the Eidra companies after the programme. Unparalleled learning opportunities, through work and trainings, mentoring, coaching. A career platform that supports long-term growth. Home base in Eidra House, with barista service, gym, gaming room and restaurants. Benefits including insurance, gym sponsorship and social activities.
