**Positioning:** the interdisciplinary hire the posting is describing when it says "a designer who thrives at the intersection of design and business", except he arrives with the consulting already done.

**Profile chosen:** `profile.proptech`, the block default, deliberately left alone. It opens on computational design and data analysis, names Python, QGIS, machine learning and Grasshopper, and closes on building data tools that turn spatial and financial data into decisions. For a collective that defines itself as consulting, creativity, engineering and data, that hybrid is the pitch, and dressing it up as a pure analytics profile would throw away the half that makes him distinctive here.

**Bucket:** `target/`. Consulting with a data core is a direction `notes/context.md` explicitly endorses for the short and medium term, and this is a level he can realistically get. The caveat below is about placement, not about whether to apply.

**What is different from every other CV in this repo:** the creative and design strands stay in. The deployable-structures workshop at Southeast University, the Grasshopper work, and the `digital_tools` group (Rhino, Revit, Adobe) all survive, where the EY-Parthenon and LEGO applications cut them. Eidra owns Kurppa Hosk and Animal alongside Curamando; a candidate who can hold design and analysis in the same head is the point, not a compromise.

**Threads to lead with:**

1. **He is the archetype, literally.** They list "a designer who thrives at the intersection of design and business" and "an engineer who thinks like a strategist" as two of the people they want. He trained as an architect, ran his own studio's commercials, and now builds machine learning models. The letter should not argue this. It should show it and let the reader notice.
2. **"Bonus if you have work experience" undersells what he has.** Ramboll Management Consulting and Sweco are consultancies with real clients, tenders and deadlines. Four and a half years, four countries, work for the World Bank, EBRD, KfW and EIB. Against a graduate field this is the strongest card and it costs nothing to play plainly.
3. **Curious, creative, structured, collaborative, adaptable.** These are the five words the posting uses, and each has a concrete answer: selected for a two-week international workshop in Nanjing (curious); a construction technique from seashells and algae that became the school's Venice Biennale installation with CITA, GXN and 3XN (creative); tender methodology and bid pricing under deadline at Sweco (structured); a tool he taught into adoption across 10+ projects (collaborative); Brazil to Denmark to Azerbaijan, four working languages (adaptable). Pick two or three, do not list all five.
4. **The rotation is the actual product.** He should say why a year of moving between companies suits him specifically: his own career has been a sequence of deliberate lateral moves toward the same question, and this is that pattern with an institution behind it.

**Open flags for Antonio:**

- **Confirm the location.** Eidra House is Stockholm. `notes/context.md` treats the Nordics as one bloc and Stockholm as a genuine relocation option, so this is fine, but it is a move.

The permanent role comes after the 12 months. Landing on the marketing side would repeat the LEGO problem. Use the rotation to push toward the consulting companies and be explicit about it in interviews.
