# Research Analyst, Germany — Cushman & Wakefield

Location: Germany (city not stated in the posting). Reports to the Research Team Lead, Germany, and works with the wider EMEA research function.

## Role

The Research Analyst is responsible for research, data and analytics and reporting activities across the German market. The role is designed to support the organic growth of the German businesses through thought leadership and insight outputs that help generate leads and opportunities. It reports to and works closely with the Research Team Lead, Germany, to ensure consistency of client experience, processes and operational excellence across EMEA, and cooperates with local business services departments and central functions, mainly Marketing and Data & Analytics.

## Key responsibilities

- Ensure real estate data collection and update of existing databases (office, industrial, retail, residential, hotel, investment) to deliver regular market and economic updates, with focus areas determined by team structure, location and other factors.
- Cooperate with other business services teams to gather, analyse and use local and regional market insights, including competitive, client and industry data, to produce market thought leadership.
- Support business development activities, including presenting market updates to clients.
- Undertake independent market analyses for ad-hoc client reports as defined by the business.
- Support marketing and communication activities in line with the company content strategy.
- Contribute to C&W brand visibility through participation in public events and social media.
- Cooperate with C&W research teams in other countries and the wider EMEA team.

## Knowledge and experience

- Strong analytical skills with expertise in data collection, statistical analysis and market research.
- Ability to interpret data, identify trends and assess market dynamics with accuracy.
- Excellent communication skills for translating complex data into clear reports, presentations and visualizations.
- Creative problem-solving and effective collaboration in dynamic, deadline-driven environments.
- Proficiency in MS Office (Excel, Word, PowerPoint, OneDrive, SharePoint); familiarity with Power BI, Power Query and GIS tools (ArcGIS, QGIS).
- Experience in professional services or real estate sector, aligned with role seniority.
- Degree in Economics, Geography, Real Estate or related field preferred.
