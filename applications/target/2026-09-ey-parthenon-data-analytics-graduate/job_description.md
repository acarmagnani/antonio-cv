# Graduate, Data & Analytics, EY-Parthenon, Denmark

**Company:** EY-Parthenon (Data & Analytics, Nordic-wide team)
**Location:** Denmark (Nordic-wide projects)
**Deadline:** 11 October 2026
**Note:** the Danish sibling of the Helsinki trainee posting in `applications/wide/2026-08-ey-parthenon-data-analytics-trainee/`. Same team, separate country intake.

How will you contribute to shaping a better future, together?

Are you ready to kick-start your career in a collaborative and high-performing environment? Join our Data and Analytics team at EY-Parthenon and work alongside experienced colleagues on challenging and meaningful projects from day one.

## The opportunity: your next adventure awaits

As a Graduate in the Data & Analytics team, you will help our clients uncover the key drivers behind their businesses by combining analytics, technology, and commercial understanding. Working across mergers, acquisitions, and strategic initiatives, you will analyze large and complex datasets, develop innovative solutions, and apply advanced analytics and AI to answer critical business questions and support strategic decision-making.

As part of a Nordic-wide team with diverse backgrounds spanning data science, engineering, finance, and accounting, you will have the opportunity to work on projects across the Nordic countries, working side by side with talented colleagues who will support and guide you from day one.

## What we look for

- You have a dynamic, growth-focused mindset. We welcome applicants of all genders and backgrounds who bring curiosity, collaboration, and a desire to grow. How you absorb information and take action matters as much as your education and experience. Varied perspectives make our team stronger.
- You are curious and purpose driven. You see opportunities, ask insightful questions, and have a genuine interest in finance, M&A, technology and analytics, with a curiosity for emerging innovations and technology trends.
- Graduating in 2027 or have recently completed a relevant master's degree. We welcome applications from a variety of academic backgrounds. Relevant fields may include Economics, Finance, Data Science and Engineering.

### Qualifications

- An analytical mindset and a genuine enjoyment of tackling complex problems.
- Ability to collaborate effectively in teams with people from varying technical and personal backgrounds.
- Strong communication and presentation skills.
- Experience/knowledge of programming language, data modelling, visualization and AI/ML using tools such as Python, SQL, Power BI, Fabric, and LLMs such as ChatGPT, Copilot and Claude.

## What's in it for you

Attractive compensation package with comprehensive insurance package, pension and employee benefits. An exciting introductory program, connecting you with fellow graduates across the Nordics. Personalized support and mentorship through a dedicated buddy and counselor invested in your success. Flexible and hybrid-working conditions. Continuous learning and development opportunities.

## Recruitment process

1. **Application:** upload CV and latest grade transcript.
2. **Assessment:** assessment link within a few days of applying, about 20 minutes.
3. **Video:** a short video about your motivation for the role.
4. **Interview:** two rounds of face-to-face interviews for selected candidates, including one case interview, in November and December 2026.

Application deadline: 11 October 2026.
