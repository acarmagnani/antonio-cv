# Management Consultant (Junior Consultant, graduate) — Rystad Energy, Advisory — Oslo, Norway

Do you want to challenge yourself as a Management Consultant in one of the world's leading energy knowledge houses?

## About Rystad Energy

Rystad Energy is a leading global independent research and energy intelligence company dedicated to helping clients navigate the future of energy. By providing high-quality data and thought leadership, our international team empowers businesses, governments and organizations to make well-informed decisions.

Our extensive portfolio of products and solutions covers all aspects of global energy fundamentals, spanning every corner of the oil and gas industry, renewables, clean technologies, supply chain and power markets. Headquartered in Oslo, Norway, with an expansive global network, our advisory, data and analysis services provide clients a competitive edge in the market.

## Our Management Consulting offering

The Advisory department works with energy industry stakeholders on challenges relating to strategy, market fundamentals and value chain development within oil and gas, power, and renewable energy to help them make higher quality decisions based on unique insights. Our engagements span a broad set of topics and client types from commercial due diligence / transaction support for investors, to market assessment support for cleantech start-ups, corporate strategy development for major energy companies, and policy design for governmental bodies.

We are seeking graduates for junior consultant positions in Oslo to support our growing business in Europe and the Middle East. We are looking for highly motivated individuals with a strong interest in the energy sector, consulting, and the Energy Transition.

## Key Responsibilities

You will work on challenging assignments requiring quantitative and qualitative analysis to drive insights and conclusions. As a member of project teams, you will engage closely with clients to shape their decisions and strategies. You will travel to client sites as required but work primarily at Rystad Energy's premises.
