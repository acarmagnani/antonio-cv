# Positioning, Rystad Energy (junior Management Consultant, Advisory, Oslo)

**One line:** an analyst who already works inside international energy and infrastructure advisory, doing the quantitative and qualitative work that lenders and investors act on, now applying it to energy markets.

**Profile chosen:** `profile.infrastructure-consulting`. It is the only variant that names energy projects and international consulting up front, and it lands climate risk assessment and GIS analysis as method rather than as sustainability advocacy.

**Threads to lead with:**

1. **Sweco, energy and water infrastructure advisory for development banks.** This is the closest thing in his record to what the Advisory department does: capital-intensive energy and water assets, appraised to World Bank, EBRD, KfW and EIB standards, with climate risk rated asset by asset under JASPERS and EBRD guidance so the finding drives an investment and adaptation decision. Consulting delivery, client and counterpart engagement, tender methodology and a pricing model behind bid decisions.
2. **Ramboll, market and supply chain analysis for the energy transition.** The critical raw materials study mapped 20+ supply chains under the EU Critical Raw Materials Act, profiling producing companies, sectors and geographies from industry databases: market fundamentals research of exactly the kind Rystad sells. Alongside it, peer benchmarking for a corporate client, automated risk tooling in Excel, and AI research tools integrated into due diligence workflows, which the posting asks for by name.

**The energy transition angle:** he is not a generalist looking for consulting, he is someone whose whole record sits on the physical and regulatory side of the transition (energy assets, raw materials, building energy performance, climate risk) and who wants to work on where that transition actually goes.

**Known gap, not to be papered over:** no oil and gas or power market modelling, and no commercial due diligence on a transaction. The letter does not claim either; it leads with analytical method, energy exposure, and the interest in the sector.
