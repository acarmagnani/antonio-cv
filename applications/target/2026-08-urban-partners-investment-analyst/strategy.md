# Strategy, Urban Partners Investment Analyst (Nordic Credit)

## Positioning

Antonio has spent several years judging whether a real estate project holds up under its own
numbers, from the developer's side. He is moving to the capital side of the same question.

## Profile chosen

`profile.proptech-real-estate`. It leads with feasibility and financial analysis, names
predictive modeling and machine learning applied to property and market data, and closes on

## Threads to lead with

1. **Underwriting logic, already practised.** OSPA is the anchor: 15+ feasibility studies
   across Brazil and the US, sizing investment and return against buildable area, unit mix,
   price per square metre and parking, plus reading master plans to find where regulation left
   buildable value unclaimed. That is the same question the Credit team asks, from the other
   side of the table.

2. **Quantitative depth.** House price prediction (ensemble learning on structured property and
   financial data, top 2%) and the ML development recommendation tool. These beat a generic
   "strong analytical skills" claim.

3. **Lender-facing and due diligence exposure.** The Sweco anchor names World Bank, EBRD, KfW
   and EIB financed projects, the climate risk chapters went to client and lender review, and
   the engineering design review is third-party report review, which the JD asks for
   explicitly. Itau is due diligence on buildings a bank was acquiring or leasing.

4. **Sector credibility on the ESG axis.** The Ramboll Genesta engagement was for real estate
   investment managers. Urban Partners leads on sustainable cities, so this reads as an asset
   rather than a detour.
