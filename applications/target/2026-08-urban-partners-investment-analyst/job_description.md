# Investment Analyst, Nordic Credit team

**Company:** Urban Partners (formerly NREP)
**Location:** Stockholm, SE
**Team:** Nordic Credit, part of a wider Credit team of ~20 across Stockholm, Copenhagen,
Frankfurt, Munich and London.

## About

Urban Partners is a leading European urban investment manager, owner and operator with a
20-year track record of delivering strong, risk-adjusted returns through market cycles. We
develop, finance and actively manage high-quality assets in prime locations across Northern
Europe. Strong focus on investing to create sustainable cities for the future.

## The role

Investment Analyst supporting financing solutions for high-quality real estate projects across
the Nordics. Working closely with a Vice President and/or Associate across the full investment
lifecycle, from screening and underwriting to execution and portfolio monitoring.

## Responsibilities

- Supporting the execution of credit investments across the full lifecycle from initial
  screening to closing and ongoing monitoring
- Building and maintaining financial models, including cash flow analysis, to assess
  profitability, debt capacity, and risk
- Performing detailed underwriting of real estate assets and development projects to identify
  the investments offering the most attractive risk-adjusted returns
- Conducting and coordinating due diligence processes, including collaboration with external
  advisors and review of third-party reports
- Analysing micro and macro market data, market trends, and sector dynamics to support
  investment decisions
- Preparing high-quality investment materials and presentations for internal committees and
  external stakeholders, including fund investors
- Supporting the structuring of financing solutions, including term sheets and the loan
  documentation process
- Supporting portfolio monitoring, reporting, and investor deliverables, including input to
  fund reporting and capital raising activities

## Experience & skills

Ideally around 1-2 years of relevant experience, but they will also consider strong applicants
graduating now. **Open to candidates from a wide range of educational backgrounds, and they
explicitly encourage those with strong results in non-finance related programs and a strong
desire to learn to apply.**

- Strong English. **At least a conversational level of Swedish.** Another Nordic language a plus.
- Curious and eager to learn, genuine interest in real estate and credit
- Strong analytical and communication skills
- Comfortable working with numbers
- Proactive team player, humble and easy to work with

## Known gaps

- **Swedish: none.** This is the main obstacle. Stated as expected, though phrased more softly
  than an absolute requirement.
- No credit experience: term sheets, debt capacity, loan documentation. They hire at 1-2 years
  and train, so this is expected to be learnable.
- No formal finance education, which the posting explicitly opens the door on.
