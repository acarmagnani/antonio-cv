## Original posting (English version as published)

Become a Graduate in PwC Consulting and Step Into the Role of Consultant

Would you like to start your career in a role that exposes you to a wide variety of projects and challenges? And would you like the opportunity to shape your consulting career within a field that matches your interests and ambitions? Then join PwC's Consulting graduate programme. Here, you'll find a world of opportunities, a high level of professional excellence, and a strong sense of community, all designed to support your growth both professionally and personally.

"As a graduate, you get the best possible start to your consulting career. At the same time, you become part of a strong community of fellow graduates and have the opportunity to build a network across PwC. This creates a solid foundation for your development, both professionally and personally," says Emily, People & Culture Lead in Consulting.

**Get Off to a Great Start**

On 1 September 2027, you will begin your graduate journey in Consulting, joining our department and a strong team of talented colleagues with diverse backgrounds. From day one, you will become an integrated member of your team and quickly take on responsibility in exciting client engagements, learning through hands-on experience and close collaboration with experienced consultants. You will be permanently employed in the department, and over the next two years, you will be prepared for a consulting career through practical experience, sparring with experienced colleagues, and a tailored development programme. In addition, you will be assigned your own Career Coach, who will support your professional and personal development throughout the graduate programme and beyond as your career progresses at PwC.

**Dive Into the Role of a Consultant**

As a graduate, you will join one of our Consulting teams, where you will work closely with clients and colleagues from day one to solve complex business challenges. No two days are the same as a consultant. You will analyse challenges, develop solutions, and facilitate workshops. At the same time, you will have the opportunity to build deep expertise within the area that interests you the most. When applying, we therefore ask you to indicate which of the following areas you would like to join and where you believe your profile is the best fit.

- **Strategy & Transformation Excellence:** Would you like to help organisations define strategies across both the private and public sectors and lead the transformations that bring those strategies to life? We work with strategy development, programme and project management, and business transformation across organisations' operational value chains. In addition, we have specialists with particular expertise in the energy sector, defence and defence industries, as well as the healthcare and pharmaceutical sectors.
- **Technology, AI & Data Excellence:** Would you like to shape the organisations of the future and turn emerging technologies into real business value? We advise both private companies and public-sector organisations on transforming complex digital and technological ambitions into tangible business outcomes. You will work at the intersection of technology, strategy, business, and innovation, helping to create modern, scalable, and future-proof solutions that empower organisations to succeed.
- **Cyber Excellence:** Would you like to help organisations protect their businesses in an increasingly digital world? We advise clients on cyber and information security, digital risk, compliance, and resilient security solutions. Our work ranges from security strategy and GRC to the implementation of technical and organisational security measures.
- **Risk Excellence:** Would you like to work with risk management and help build trust in complex organisations? We support clients with governance, compliance, regulation, treasury, fraud investigations, and the development of robust control environments, enabling them to navigate a dynamic world with increasing risks and growing expectations for transparency and accountability.

**A Strong Community And Great Colleagues Who Become Friends**

In Consulting, we combine professional excellence with a strong sense of community. We believe the best results are achieved through collaboration, knowledge sharing, and continuous sparring. You will take on responsibility early in your career, but you will never stand alone. Whether you are working on-site with clients or on internal projects, you will always be surrounded by experienced colleagues who are committed to helping you develop both professionally and personally. At the same time, you will become part of PwC's graduate community across the firm, participating in onboarding activities, networking events, and development programmes alongside fellow graduates.

**Are You Finishing Your Master's Degree and Ready for the Next Step?**

Seize the opportunity and start the graduate journey that suits you best. As long as you hold, or are about to complete, a Master's degree, we are open to your educational background. Your curiosity and desire to continuously learn are expected to be your greatest strengths. Equally important is your ability to build relationships and your mindset as a team player who views solving challenges as a collective effort.

- Indicate whether you have a preference for working in Hellerup or Aarhus
