# Analyst, Economics and Transition Finance, Mærsk Mc-Kinney Møller Center for Zero Carbon Shipping

**Company:** Mærsk Mc-Kinney Møller Center for Zero Carbon Shipping (not-for-profit, independent R&D center)
**Team:** Economics and Transition Finance, within the Regulation and Policy Hub
**Location:** Copenhagen, Denmark. Full-time, permanent. On-site, no hybrid, residence in or near Copenhagen required. Occasional travel.
**Source:** posting pasted 2026-09-07

## Who We Are

The Mærsk Mc-Kinney Møller Center for Zero Carbon Shipping is a not-for-profit, independent R&D center working to accelerate the decarbonization of the global maritime industry. In close collaboration with partners across shipping, energy, academia, and authorities, we develop pathways, test new technologies, and de-risk critical components of the transition.

We value the diversity of our talent in all its forms, and we see diversity as a genuine source of strength for building high-performing teams. We welcome international candidates who are willing to relocate to join our mission.

## The Opportunity

The regulatory landscape for shipping is evolving rapidly. Governments and regulators across the globe are introducing new measures to reduce greenhouse gas emissions from shipping, while industrial policy and energy security concerns are shaping the next generation of fuels and technologies. Decisions being made today through initiatives such as the IMO Net-Zero Framework, FuelEU Maritime, and the EU ETS will play a critical role in determining how the maritime energy transition unfolds.

To support the development of effective and evidence-based policies, we are looking for an Analyst to join our Economics and Transition Finance team within the Regulation and Policy Hub. In this role, you will help build the analytical foundation that supports policy proposals and assessments for governments, industry stakeholders, and civil society organizations.

As the team's dedicated analytical resource, you will work directly with Navigate, the Center's integrated assessment model, as well as other analytical tools and datasets to evaluate policy impacts and market developments. You will combine modelling, data analysis, and policy research to generate robust evidence and actionable insights that contribute to international maritime decarbonization efforts.

This is a unique opportunity to influence some of the most important policy discussions shaping the future of global shipping.

## What You'll Do

- Model the impact of regulatory designs, assessing different policy mechanisms, scenarios, and fuel cost assumptions while ensuring transparency and reproducibility of results.
- Analyse and interpret large datasets, including EU MRV, IMO DCS, fuel market data, and shipping market data.
- Conduct research across academic literature, regulatory submissions, and industry publications to support policy development and recommendations.
- Develop analytical briefs, position papers, IMO submissions, presentations, and contributions to the Center's publications.
- Investigate related policy frameworks and real-world case studies, extracting lessons relevant to maritime decarbonization.
- Present findings and recommendations to internal leadership, governments, partner organizations, and industry stakeholders.
- Support policy projects from initial scoping and research through analysis, writing, and stakeholder reporting.
- Lead and coordinate analytical deliverables involving multiple contributors, tight timelines, and close collaboration across teams, including Communications and Engagement.
