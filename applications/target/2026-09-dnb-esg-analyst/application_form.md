# DNB application form answers

Free-text fields in DNB's form, hard limit of 200 characters each (Norwegian "tegn"). Answered in English.

**2. What do you consider to be the most interesting responsibilities / tasks in this position?** (180 characters)

Building the data quality monitoring and dashboards that make issues visible, and acting as the bridge between what ESG reporting requires and how the pipelines are actually built.

I want ESG work where the figures are controlled and actually used. In GRM the data steers a portfolio and meets regulators, which is the standard I worked to at Sweco and Ramboll.
