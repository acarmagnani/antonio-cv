# Research Analyst, Research & Insight — Montagu Evans, London

**Company:** Montagu Evans (UK property consultancy)
**Team:** Research & Insight (newly formed), Central Management Team, led by Jon Neale
**Location:** London, UK

---

Montagu Evans is a UK property consultancy that helps clients unlock value from complexity, because that's where the most meaningful opportunities live. Our purpose is to bring clarity, creativity, and conviction to the complex. We are experts who collaborate to create value, not just preserve it, and our team is built for impact, not oversight. We specialise in high-value, complex assets across the property lifecycle, where political, technical or commercial challenges demand a joined-up approach.

## The Team / Department

This role is to sit within the newly formed Research & Insight team, working closely with Jon Neale, who leads the team. It provides market-leading research and data to the business and its clients. The topic matter can range from the state of play in individual property markets and the demographics of particular places to the impact of global megatrends and economics on the sectors we work in. We aim to provide our clients and the wider market with original insight that helps guide and shape business decisions and draws attention to Montagu Evans' offer, alongside providing direct advisory products to clients and supporting the business with essential background, facts and data.

## Role Overview

Montagu Evans is seeking a creative and insightful research analyst to help improve our proprietary data sources, providing market and economic reporting, and develop original insight to inform clients and internal stakeholders. The role will be based in our Central Management Team. It will be highly visible within the firm and offers excellent opportunities for career development and progression, working within a dynamic and fast-growing team.

### Key responsibilities

- Analysis and reporting on key market areas and wider themes.
- Helping to develop Montagu Evans' proprietary market datasets, with a focus on industrial & logistics, residential and Central London.
- Managing and interrogating third party datasets and maintaining our suite of economic and market slides.
- Contributing to pitches and client presentations, requests and reports where appropriate.

### Wider responsibilities

- Responding to requests from the business for support.
- Contributing to pitches, presentations and client reports.
- Liaising with stakeholders and the wider Clients, Brand and Markets Team.
- Developing new ways to visualise data and explain trends.

## The successful candidate should have

- A degree, preferably in a relevant subject (such as Economics, Geography, Real Estate or Planning).
- At least 1-2 years' post-graduation experience in a similar role.
- Excellent analytical, report writing and presentational skills, as well as strong commercial awareness and the ability to work to challenging deadlines.
- A knowledge of, or interest in, the UK's urban geography, urban economics and/or the property market.

## Advantageous

- Sourcing of relevant datasets and information.
- Microsoft Excel (intermediate to advanced is essential).
- GIS (QGIS preferred) and spatial data analysis skills (training can be provided).
- Statistical languages such as R or Python (not essential).
