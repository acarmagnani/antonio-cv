Dear Hiring Team at Montagu Evans,

I trained as an architect in Brazil and spent most of that time drawn to the questions that come before a design brief. Why is this being built here, who is actually going to be nearby in ten years, what does the regulation quietly permit that nobody has noticed. Those are research questions rather than architectural ones, which is eventually why I left São Paulo for Copenhagen and an M.Arch. in Architecture Computation at The Royal Danish Academy, a programme about using data to understand real estate and cities. A newly formed Research and Insight team whose remit runs from individual market conditions to demographics and megatrends is a rare place to do that properly.

My master's thesis was a computational analysis of underused space in the historic centre of São Paulo, combining urban morphology, land use data and social vulnerability indicators in QGIS, then building an algorithm that generated intensification typologies tested against the city's own planning policy. Assembling that dataset was most of the work, and it is the part I would bring to improving proprietary market data here. Earlier, at OSPA, I read municipal master plans and land-use law across fifteen feasibility studies to establish what each site could actually support, so the regulatory layer is familiar territory rather than a new subject.

Alongside that I built a machine learning tool that generates development recommendations for buildings in Copenhagen from financial, social, sustainability and geospatial data, with an interface letting different stakeholders reweight the drivers, and a house price prediction model that finished in the top two percent of its competition. At Sweco I ran GIS across the office, producing the cartographic material behind methodology and proposal documents, and at Ramboll I led an internal market study mapping critical raw materials supply chains to identify new service lines.

I do not yet know the UK market, and I would be learning it. What I do know is how to read a city through its geography, its regulation and its data, and that method travels. Montagu Evans works on exactly the complex, contested assets where it earns its keep.

All the best,
Antonio Carmagnani
