# Strategy, Constanter Investment Office, Real Assets (entry level), Zug

**Positioning:** the rare entry-level candidate who has already worked on two of their three real asset classes from the asset side, real estate and internationally financed infrastructure, and who comes with the responsible-investment layer already built in rather than bolted on.

**Profile selected:** `profile.real-estate-esg-data` (override of the block's default).

**Block:** `real-estate-investment`, plus the two Sweco field-mission bullets, minus the two most delivery-flavoured architecture bullets. See `selection.yaml`.

**Threads to lead with:**

- **The real assets double is the whole argument.** Real estate through OSPA, Isay Weinfeld, Felipe Hess and Itaú-Unibanco (feasibility, buildable capacity, and technical and environmental due diligence on buildings a bank was acquiring or leasing), and infrastructure through Sweco, on capital-intensive water, energy and transport assets financed by the World Bank, EBRD, KfW and EIB, worked to the appraisal and reporting standards those lenders require. Candidates with one of those are common. Candidates with both, at entry level, are not.
- **He has been on the asset tour.** The Baku field mission is literal, on-the-ground asset-condition assessment across a water supply system, with technical, social, environmental and human rights risks identified through site inspections and interviews, plus engagement with national agencies to align on rehabilitation priorities. That maps directly onto attending asset tours and engaging external managers and third parties.
- **Responsible investment is not a side interest.** Ramboll due diligence for real estate investment clients, review of a real estate investment manager's Code of Conduct, EU Taxonomy and CSRD, and physical climate risk rated asset by asset under JASPERS and EBRD climate-proofing standards. Constanter states that returns and sustainability go hand in hand, and this is the part of the CV that shows he can actually do the tracking and monitoring their Responsible Investment colleagues need.
- **Tools they named.** Strong Excel including a fully automated risk-assessment system consolidating data into a live dashboard, an Excel and VBA analysis tool, Python (including a property valuation model in the top 2% of its competition), Power BI and SQL. Python is an explicit advantage in the posting.

**The honest gaps:** no cash flow modelling, no underwriting, no investment bank or asset manager internship, and the master's is an M.Arch rather than Economics or Finance. Do not dress any of it up. The posting says "develop underwriting skills", which is an invitation to be taught, so the letter argues readiness to learn from a real analytical base, not fluency he lacks.

**Relocation, to be met head on:** the posting says the candidate must have full working rights and be based in Switzerland. He is Italian, so the EU-Swiss free movement agreement gives him the right to a B permit on a job offer, but he is in Copenhagen today. The letter states the citizenship and the willingness to relocate in one plain sentence rather than leaving the recruiter to guess.

**Language:** English.
