# Constanter Investment Office, Real Assets (entry level)

**Company:** Constanter (umbrella for the Porticus and Laudes foundations, founded and supported by the Brenninkmeijer family)
**Location:** Zug, Switzerland (hybrid)
**Reports to:** Head of Real Assets
**Source:** posting text pasted 2026-08-25

## About the organisation

Constanter is one of Europe's largest independent philanthropic organizations, established to promote respect for human dignity, sustainability and social justice. The Constanter Investment Office manages the foundation's endowment, with the goal of supporting philanthropic giving in perpetuity. It invests across public, private and real assets using a Total Portfolio approach that seeks superior risk-adjusted returns, with a long term orientation built on strong partnerships. It looks for companies and funds that take their environmental, social and governance responsibilities seriously.

## About the role

Entry level position supporting the Real Assets team, which invests globally across real estate, infrastructure and natural resources. Support the evolution, development and implementation of the real asset investment strategy. Manage, monitor and report on existing investments to key stakeholders. Support the sourcing, analysis and evaluation of real asset investment opportunities in pooled funds, co-investments, separate accounts and public markets. Mentored within a 4 person team. Exposure to a multi-asset class portfolio through the Total Portfolio Approach.

## Key responsibilities

- Support the management of existing assets within the Real Assets Portfolio. Where necessary, attend manager meetings and asset tours and ensure assets are responsibly managed to maximise overall performance. Support the buy/hold/sell analysis for existing assets. Support Real Asset team members in asset management and diligence initiatives, cash flow modelling, reviewing key fundamentals, and tracking operating and market measures.
- Support the tracking and monitoring of new investment opportunities. Overall responsibility for maintenance of Real Asset pipeline tracking.
- Review market research and pricing within real estate, infrastructure and natural resources. Contribute to discussion on market dynamics. Support the maintenance of and build market pricing trackers, benchmarking analysis tools, and relative value measurement to enable assessment of real asset sub sectors.
- Develop underwriting skills across direct, co-investment, and fund opportunities, reviewing JV- and operating partner financial models and stress-testing their business plans, financial models, and key assumptions.
- Support investment due diligence on new investment proposals. Support initial screening of viable investment opportunities including presentations within the Real Asset team. Support ad hoc diligence workstreams across Real Assets as required.
- Work with Responsible Investment colleagues to ensure the Real Asset investments impacts are tracked and monitored.
- Investment Reporting: draft and produce reporting as required across the real asset investments and investment portfolio as a whole.
