# Development Associate, Greystar (Spain)

Greystar is a leading, fully integrated global real estate platform offering expertise in property management, investment management, development, and construction services in institutional-quality rental housing. Headquartered in Charleston, South Carolina, Greystar manages and operates over $350 billion of real estate in more than 260 markets globally with offices throughout North America, Europe, South America, and the Asia-Pacific region. Greystar is the largest operator of apartments in the United States, managing over one million units/beds globally. Across its platforms, Greystar has nearly $79 billion of assets under management, including over $34 billion of development assets and over $36.5 billion of regulatory assets under management. Greystar was founded by Bob Faith in 1993 to become a provider of world-class service in the rental residential real estate business. To learn more, visit www.greystar.com.

## JOB DESCRIPTION SUMMARY

The Development Associate will contribute to both investment analysis and development management, supporting the assessment, acquisition, and delivery of living projects across Spain. This role offers broad exposure across financial modelling, market research, development feasibility, planning processes, cost control, and reporting to senior management.

## JOB DESCRIPTION

### Key Role Responsibilities

**Financial Analysis & Investment**

- Conduct detailed financial modelling, valuation, and feasibility analysis of potential acquisitions and development opportunities.
- Develop and interpret complex financial models including multi-tier waterfall structures.
- Assist in budgeting, cost control, and cash flow monitoring of development projects throughout the lifecycle.
- Prepare financial and technical data required for investment memorandums, including detailed pro-forma construction budgets and feasibility studies.
- Support the negotiation of sale and purchase agreements with financial and technical analysis.

**Development & Project Support**

- Support the planning and approvals process, including analysis of planning permissions, zoning regulations, and compliance with Spain development control requirements.
- Assist in the preparation and monitoring of project plans, programme schedules, and cost documentation.
- Monitor budget and project schedule performance and contribute to regular progress reporting.
- Support the management of third-party relationships with consultants, contractors, and external stakeholders to ensure project delivery.
- Assist in ensuring all required permits and approvals are obtained and that technical quality standards are met.

**Reporting & Presentations**

- Prepare investment papers, market reports, and presentations for internal committees and senior stakeholders.
- Contribute to project internal reporting and approval presentations (DCSR, Pre-Flight, BLIC).
- Produce meeting minutes, progress reports, management reports, and quality reports as required.

**Market Research & Strategy**

- Conduct market research and sector analysis, identifying trends and opportunities in residential real estate.
- Maintain awareness of Flex Living, Build to Rent (BTR), and PBSA market dynamics in Spain.
- Contribute to the broader strategic objectives of development projects.
