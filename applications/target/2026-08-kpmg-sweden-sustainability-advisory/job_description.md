# Sustainability Advisory (graduate) — KPMG Sweden, Stockholm

**Company:** KPMG Sweden
**Team:** Sustainability Advisory, ~20 people, inside Transformation & Risk Consulting (~140), part of the Nordic ESG community and global ESG network

---

Are you passionate about contributing to the sustainable development of corporates and society? Become a member of KPMG's Sustainability Advisory!

When working at KPMG Sustainability Advisory you will be part of a global organization and work within various industries and areas, together with competent and inspiring colleagues within the Swedish organization and other member firms.

In our Sustainability Advisory team, you'll find plenty of opportunities to collaborate in an inclusive environment, where everyone is encouraged to bring unique and diverse perspectives into the work that we do for our clients. We work side by side in a well-composed team, combining sustainability expertise with traditional consulting skills.

We make an impact for our clients in a broad range of topics including sustainability strategy and value creation, climate change mitigation and adaptation, biodiversity, circular business models, **human rights and sustainable supply chains**, sustainability governance, **sustainability in valuations**, and technology enablement. With sustainability increasingly being raised as a key strategic topic for our clients, a role in the sustainability team offers numerous opportunities for your personal and professional development.

## The team

As a member of Sustainability Advisory, you will be part of a focused local team of approximately 20 people. The team sits within KPMG's broader Transformation & Risk Consulting area, a community of around 140 colleagues, and is also part of the Nordic ESG community and global ESG network. You will collaborate closely with colleagues across complementary capabilities, including Corporate Services, Technology, Capital Markets, Assurance, and other advisory teams.

## What do we offer?

Together with other KPMG colleagues, you will be working on exciting client engagements. During your first years at KPMG Management Consulting, you will be a part of our learning and development program. In the program, you will acquire knowledge about business sectors, industry trends, and KPMG's customers. You will learn about our tools and methods, gain deep knowledge about our capabilities and delivery models, while also improving your leadership and sales skills. Furthermore, you will get a mentor and a dedicated performance manager supporting you in your career.

## We are looking for

We are looking for dedicated recent or soon to be graduates who strive to achieve excellence and are looking for a great career path. More specifically, we want you to have:

- A university degree in business, management, finance, economics, or engineering, where a focus on sustainability is meriting.
- A strong business and digital mindset.
- Excellent communication skills.
- Multilingual proficiency (fluency in English is required, Swedish is an advantage).
- Analytical and problem-solving skills.
- Excellent academic results.
- A team player attitude with a genuine interest in seeing customers grow and being part of their journey.

We also value international experience, previous work experience, and other engagements and achievements, showing a strong drive, high ambition, and passion.
