Dear Hiring Team at Ørsted,

I trained as an architect, but what has held my attention since is the part of the built environment that decides things, the documents and the numbers behind a building rather than its drawings. That interest moved me from São Paulo to Copenhagen for an M.Arch in Architecture Computation at The Royal Danish Academy, and from design work toward portfolios, contracts and the data that describes them. A lease management role inside a global real estate team is where those two halves of my background stop being separate.

At Sweco I supported the setup of a new branch office in Bangladesh, drafting the employment contracts and managing the full insurance onboarding, which meant working through commercial terms and obligations that somebody would later be held to. Alongside that I worked on internationally financed water and energy projects, where I engaged national agencies and government counterparts to align priorities and collect the data the projects ran on. Being the point of contact between parties who need different things from the same document is familiar work, and I like it more than I expected to.

Earlier, at Itaú-Unibanco, I assessed the performance of buildings the bank was acquiring or leasing and reviewed technical and regulatory documentation as a compliance gate before construction could be authorised. I also built an Excel and VBA dashboard tracking the bank's corporate inventory across its locations, replacing a manual process and speeding up procurement for new-building fit-outs. At Ramboll I built an automated risk-assessment system in Excel that consolidated scattered data into a single dashboard that updated itself. Portfolio data only stays useful if somebody is stubborn about validating it, and that has been my job more than once.

Ørsted manages its offices as a global platform inside a company built around the energy transition, which is the combination I have been aiming at since I came to Denmark. That is where I want to put this next.

All the best,
Antonio Carmagnani
