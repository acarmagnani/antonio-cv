# Analyst, Energy Solutions — McKinsey & Company, London

**Company:** McKinsey & Company (Energy Solutions, within the Global Energy & Materials practice)
**Location:** London, UK (requires Skilled Worker sponsorship)
**Programme:** Next Gen / apprenticeship-style development, coaching and mentoring

---

Joining McKinsey means a high performance culture, apprenticeship and coaching, a global community across 65+ countries, and a competitive salary and benefits package.

## Your impact

You will help clients in the energy sector and beyond to improve operations and make strategic decisions for the future.

You will provide daily problem-solving, structuring, modeling, and analytical support for our Energy Solutions tools and analyses. You will gather information to develop these tools/benchmarks and synthesize findings into McKinsey-formatted products, such as complex models, summary reports, and standalone presentations for internal and external audiences.

You will collaborate with other experts to solve client problems, deepening your knowledge and refining your analytical and thought-partnership skills.

You will be based in the London office, joining the Energy Solutions team within McKinsey's Global Energy & Materials practice. Energy Solutions is a global leader in energy intelligence and analytics, leveraging distinctive market perspectives, cutting-edge data and technology to empower organizations across the entire energy value chain. Its consultants, analysts and data scientists transform data into actionable insights, enabling clients to make strategic, tactical and operational decisions, capture opportunities, manage risks and enhance performance.

## Your qualifications and skills

- University degree in management, engineering, economics, geosciences, petroleum engineering, or other disciplines with a quantitative or technical focus
- Experience, internships and/or full-time roles, in the oil and gas sector (upstream, midstream, or downstream), including exploration and production, refining, petrochemicals, fuel retail, lubricants or trading
- Strong interest in the energy industry, with a particular focus on oil and gas operations, energy markets, and the energy transition; familiarity with topics such as decarbonization is a plus
- Proven ability to collaborate effectively in a team environment and engage with individuals at all organizational levels
- Capacity to excel in a fast-paced, results-driven team setting, demonstrating a steep learning curve and a strong commitment to coaching and development
- Advanced problem-solving skills with a strong aptitude for working with numbers and quantitative models
- Proficiency in Excel and familiarity with Tableau
- Experience with modeling capability tools such as Python, R, or VBA is a plus
- Availability to travel
- Ability to effectively communicate complex ideas in English and local office language, both verbally and in writing
