## Never add

ISO 14001 · ISO 50001 · Energy Management · Auditing · BREEAM · DGNB · LEED · RICS ·
Property Management · Asset Management · anything naming data centres.

All of these are either credentials Antonio does not hold or domains he has not worked in. They
are the first thing an interviewer probes, and the cover letter states plainly that he has not
worked to ISO 14001 or 50001. A skills tag contradicting the letter in the same application is
the worst possible outcome.
