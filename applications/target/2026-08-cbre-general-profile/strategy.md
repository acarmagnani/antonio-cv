# Strategy, CBRE general candidate profile

## Why this folder exists

CBRE's careers site holds ONE profile (CV, cover letter, skills) reused across every role
Antonio applies to. No per-vacancy tailoring is possible. This is the company-level profile.

## Positioning

Antonio assesses buildings on financial and environmental terms at once, and has the data
skills to do both. Most candidates bring one side. CBRE is one of the few employers where the
combination is the core business rather than a compromise.

## Profile chosen

`profile.real-estate-esg-data`, authored for this. None of the five existing variants carried
real estate AND sustainability AND data together, which is exactly what a CBRE-wide profile
needs. ESG and data lead the sentence, real estate is the ground it stands on.

## Weighting

Sustainability and energy first, real estate underneath, data as the method throughout. That
order reflects where his odds are best (GWS energy and sustainability roles) without making the
profile unreadable for Research, Valuation or Project Management.

## Bridge bullets, the ones doing the most work

- **Ramboll real estate ESG offering**, centred on building energy and decarbonisation under EU
  building performance regulation. Real estate and sustainability in one line.
- **Ramboll Genesta code of conduct review** for real estate investment managers. Names a real
  estate client in an ESG engagement.
- **Itau building environmental and energy due diligence** on buildings the bank was acquiring
  or leasing. Financial decision driven by environmental data, which is the whole thesis.
- **House price prediction project**, ensemble learning for real estate valuation. Speaks
  directly to Valuation & Advisory.
- **Real estate development recommendation tool**, integrating financial, social, sustainability
  and geospatial data. Hits all three pillars in one project.

## Cover letter

Written to the company, not a vacancy. Throughline is the two-sided question: what a building
costs and what it consumes are the same question asked twice, and Antonio has worked both
sides. Names GWS specifically so it does not read as a mass letter, without naming a single
role.

## Gaps, do not paper over

ISO 14001 and 50001, data centre operations, RICS or equivalent credential, Danish and Swedish.

## Note on reuse

If Antonio later applies to a CBRE role far from this framing (pure Capital Markets, for
example), this profile is the wrong instrument and a dedicated application folder is better,
if the site ever allows it.
