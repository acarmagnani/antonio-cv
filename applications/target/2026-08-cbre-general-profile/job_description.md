# TARGET-ROLE BRIEF (not a job posting)

CBRE's careers site takes ONE candidate profile (CV, cover letter, skills) that is reused across
every role Antonio applies to. There is no per-vacancy tailoring possible, so this brief defines
the target instead of a posting.

## Company

CBRE, the world's largest commercial real estate services firm. 140,000+ employees, 100+
countries. Business lines relevant here:

- **Global Workplace Solutions (GWS)** — integrated facility management, energy and
  sustainability programs, ESG reporting for corporate occupiers. This is where the Energy
  Analyst role sits and where Antonio's odds are best.
- **Research** — market data and published analysis.
- **Valuation & Advisory Services** — appraisal and analysis of assets.
- **Capital Markets / Advisory & Transaction** — investment and leasing.
- **Property and Project Management.**

Core values: RISE (Respect, Integrity, Service, Excellence).

## Target positioning

Antonio applies as someone who can assess buildings and portfolios on financial AND
environmental terms, with the data skills to do both. CBRE is one of the few employers where
that combination is the core business rather than a compromise.

## What the profile must carry

- **Sustainability and energy**, weighted first: building energy and environmental performance,
  ESG due diligence and reporting, climate risk, LCA and embodied carbon.
- **Real estate as the ground it stands on**: development feasibility, financial modeling,
  zoning and land use, technical review of buildings.
- **Data as the method**: Excel and Power BI, Python, SQL, GIS, dashboards and analytical tools
  built and adopted.
- **Consulting behaviours**: stakeholder engagement, client-facing reporting, working inside
  external frameworks, technical documentation.

## Roles this profile should work for

Energy Analyst, Sustainability Analyst / Consultant, ESG Reporting Analyst, Research Analyst,
Data Analyst, Valuation Analyst, Junior Project Manager.

## Known gaps, do not paper over

ISO 14001 and ISO 50001, data centre operations, RICS or other real estate credentials,
Danish and Swedish.
