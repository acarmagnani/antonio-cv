# Strategy, CBRE GWS Energy Analyst

## Positioning

Antonio measures how buildings and assets actually perform, applies a structured framework to
that data, and turns the result into something the people running the asset can act on. He has
done this on buildings (Itau), on infrastructure assets (Sweco), and on supply chains (Ramboll).

## Profile chosen

`profile.esg-consulting`. It is the only variant that leads with sustainability AND the built
environment, names climate hazard and risk assessment, and closes on structured analytical
tools in Excel and Python. That is this job.

## Threads to lead with

1. **Structured assessment against an external framework.** The Sweco climate risk assessments
   are the anchor: JASPERS and EBRD climate-proofing guidance, hazards screened against
   projections data, risk rated by exposure and vulnerability for each asset, written up for
   client and lender review. That is the same muscle ISO 14001 and 50001 work needs, applied to
   a different standard.

2. **Building energy and environmental performance, specifically.** Itau-Unibanco is the most
   literal match in the whole CV: assessing the environmental and energy performance of
   buildings the bank was acquiring or leasing, reviewing consumption data, efficiency ratings,
   and certifications. Ramboll's real estate ESG work centred on building energy and
   decarbonisation under EU building performance regulation. Three studios of environmental and
   daylight analysis back it up.

3. **Reporting tools people actually use.** The Ramboll automated Excel risk system
   (consolidated data into one dynamically updating dashboard) and the Itau VBA inventory
   dashboard (replaced a manual process) both map onto monitoring and reporting KPIs in Excel
   and Power BI.

4. **Training and awareness.** The JD asks for it explicitly. The Colt workshop and training
   materials at Ramboll, plus the studio workshop that drove plugin adoption at Isay Weinfeld,
   are the evidence.
