# Energy Analyst

## About CBRE

CBRE is the world's leading company in commercial real estate and property-related services,
with more than 140,000 employees globally supporting clients in over 100 countries. In Global
Workplace Solutions (GWS), we deliver integrated facility management solutions to some of the
world's leading companies.

CBRE is a values-driven company guided by our core values RISE: Respect, Integrity, Service and
Excellence.

We are looking for an Energy Analyst to join our team supporting data centre operations and
sustainability initiatives.

## The Role

Contribute to day-to-day operations and service delivery within your assigned area or site.

- Support and manage energy and sustainability programs and projects across sites, regions, and
  datacenter campuses, including coordination of procurement activities and performance plans
- Monitor, analyze, and report on energy consumption, utilities, KPIs, and sustainability
  metrics (energy, water, waste), including preparing variance reports and identifying
  cost-saving opportunities
- Support and maintain Environmental and Energy Management Systems (ISO 14001 & ISO 50001),
  including documentation control, audits, compliance activities, and management reviews
- Identify risks, non-conformities, and improvement opportunities, and drive energy efficiency
  and sustainability initiatives across operations
- Collaborate with Microsoft operations, regional teams, and stakeholders, while supporting
  training, awareness programs, and communication related to energy and environmental standards

## Who You Are

We believe you have:

- Bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Sustainable Energy, or
  related field preferred (or equivalent experience)
- 2-3+ years of experience in energy management, sustainability programs, building operations,
  or infrastructure environments
- Experience supporting ISO 14001 and/or ISO 50001 management systems
- Knowledge of datacenter operations and energy management systems
- Ability to analyze energy data, trends, and KPIs and produce reports
- Proficiency in Microsoft Office tools (Excel, Power BI)
- Experience supporting projects and coordinating stakeholders
- Experience with compliance, documentation, and audits
- Strong communication skills and ability to engage with stakeholders

You are: service-minded, reliable, proactive and comfortable working in a dynamic setting.
