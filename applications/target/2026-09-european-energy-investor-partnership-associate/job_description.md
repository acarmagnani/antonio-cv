# Associate, Investor & Partnership Management — European Energy (Copenhagen)

Are you passionate about renewable energy and eager to work at the intersection of investor engagement and M&A? Join us in a role where your analytical skills, financial acumen, and communication abilities will directly support investor dialogues and strategic decision-making.

European Energy is a green energy company committed to driving the global transition to sustainable power. We develop, construct, and operate renewable energy solutions across solar (PV), wind, Power-to-X (PtX) and energy storage (BESS), creating long-term value for both society and our partners. Our core values are the foundation of everything we do: In it together, Trust and Courage, Ambition, Excitement, Inclusion, and Agility.

## Your new team

You will join our Investor & Client Management team, a group of seven colleagues focused on building and strengthening relationships with investors and partners to create new transaction and co-investment/partnership opportunities.

Through our investor and partnership dialogues, we work closely with teams across European Energy to drive customer-focused business development. This includes particularly close collaboration with our M&A team and executive management. Our work is grounded in ongoing market engagement and analysis, generating insights that are shared and leveraged throughout the organization.

The team is part of our Commercial organization, which takes a strategic and holistic approach to supporting European Energy's growth and commercial activities.

In this role, you will collaborate with colleagues from diverse professional backgrounds across the company. You will have the opportunity to contribute to a wide range of initiatives, shape your own development, and build expertise through meaningful responsibilities and cross-functional collaboration.

## Your new role

As an Associate in the Investor & Partnership Management team, you will play an important role in showcasing the core technologies of wind, solar and storage and respective divestment and co-investment projects within the European Energy portfolio.

Working closely with Key Account Managers (KAMs) and colleagues across the business, you will help develop compelling investor materials, support business development activities, and contribute to building and maintaining strong investor relationships. This role offers exposure to commercial transactions, strategic partnerships, and investment opportunities across our global renewable energy portfolio.

### Key tasks include

- Partner with senior leaders and executive management to shape business development proposals for current and prospective investors
- Collaborate with colleagues to develop compelling sales and business development materials
- Collaborate with executive management and senior stakeholders on business development propositions for current and prospective investors
- Maintain overview of documentation that supports all investor-oriented conversations
- Provide ad hoc support to executive management, your manager, and Key Account Management colleagues

## European Energy at a glance

European Energy is a Danish privately owned company founded in 2004 with headquarter in Copenhagen. We operate all the way from development, design, financing, construction, operation and selling of wind- and solar farms on land as well as offshore globally. As part of this, we are also very active in M&A activities across the full value chain. Further, we work with development of future energy solutions, e.g. Power-to-X. We have a solid collaboration with local stakeholders and want to involve all parts in the process.

We are now more than 970 employees with 51 nationalities represented in 21 countries. We all work for our common goal to be the most innovative partner within the green transition and to create The Power of Tomorrow, Today.

We lead with purpose. At European Energy, our leadership culture is built on six guiding principles that shape how we collaborate, make decisions, and grow together. These principles help us act responsibly and empower each other to succeed.
