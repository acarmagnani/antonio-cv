# GXN Innovation — Unsolicited Application (Copenhagen)

**NOTE: There is no posting. This file is a target-role brief written to run the tailoring
workflow, based on research into GXN's actual practice. It is not a job description from GXN.**

## The company

GXN is the innovation and research unit of 3XN, based in Copenhagen. It operates as a research
practice and strategic consultancy for the built environment. The "G" stands for Green.

## Known focus areas

- **Circular economy**: Design for Disassembly, material passports, reuse. Publications include
  "Building a Circular Future". Involved in CIRCuIT (circular construction in cities, incl.
  analysis of Danish apartment housing stock).
- **Material research**: green and bio-based materials, new material systems, applied testing.
- **Digital and computational methods**: parametric and computational design, custom tooling,
  advanced manufacturing (e.g. 3D printing research), data analysis.
- **Environmental performance**: LCA, embodied carbon, building performance.
- **Behaviour-oriented design**: how buildings shape the people using them.
- Works through interdisciplinary partnerships across material research, data analysis, biology
  and digitalisation.

## Target role profile (what this application positions for)

A computational / sustainability researcher or consultant who can:

- Build custom digital tools for design teams (Grasshopper, Python, Revit) rather than only use them.
- Run quantitative environmental analysis (LCA, embodied carbon, daylight and solar simulation).
- Work with spatial and material data (QGIS, geospatial analysis, supply chain and material flows).
- Apply machine learning and data methods to built-environment questions.
- Operate between research and delivery, with real architecture practice experience behind it.

## Direct connection

The Royal Danish Academy installation at the Venice Architecture Biennale 2025, presented at the
Arsenale in collaboration with CITA, GXN and 3XN, was built on the rammed blue biomass composite
technique Antonio developed in a semester project. He also contributed geospatial material-source
mappings (Matanzas panel) and produced the physical exhibition panels (Veneto panel).
