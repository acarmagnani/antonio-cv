# Construction Data & Research Analyst — Hines (Conceptual Construction Group, Europe)

**Company:** Hines (global real estate investment manager, $93.2bn AUM, 5,000 employees in 30 countries)
**Team:** Conceptual Construction Group, European Region, reporting to the Head of Conceptual Construction Europe
**Location:** not stated in the posting, European region with travel across Europe and exceptionally to Houston (confirm before sending)

---

## Responsibilities

The Construction Data & Research Analyst will be a member of the Conceptual Construction Group, supporting the European Region and Hines investors, working closely with the Conceptual Construction team in Europe, Asia and the Americas. The role is a critical link with the wider European Country Teams, acquiring, assimilating and managing data related to all projects from pre-investment through to completion and operation.

The analyst will be responsible for developing and maintaining a database covering construction costs, soft costs, scheduling (programme) information, lessons learnt and key project summary data compiled from all projects, and will support the team in using and developing analytical tools to interpret that data and produce forecast project costs and programmes to inform future strategic investment decisions. The role includes tracking key industry factors such as inflation and construction material costs across the European region, and supporting the team with records of key building performance data.

- Drive data collection of construction information from all projects across Europe, across a large portfolio of concurrent projects.
- Develop a database and repository for collected construction data for all projects.
- Develop a list of key metrics and critical data projections required for European projects, with the wider Conceptual Construction team.
- Develop and implement tools to use historic construction cost and schedule data for forward projections.
- Attend project meetings, understand the projects, and lead construction data collection, management and processing.
- Provide knowledge insights and relevant data to the relevant Conceptual Construction project leader.
- Provide data and benchmark detail per project, helping ensure projected schedules are attainable and costs accurate.
- Assist with standardising budget formats across projects so the data can be used in financial modelling, establishing two-way communication with the investment teams.
- Support and lead specific topics in design meetings to determine project progress and areas needing attention.
- Willingness to travel to European locations, and exceptionally to Houston, USA.

## Qualifications

- No minimum project experience required, although exposure to development projects and the construction process is highly beneficial.
- Degree qualification required in a relevant Engineering or Construction related field. A master's qualification in a related discipline may be seen as beneficial.
- Possess or working towards a formal industry qualification, such as RICS or similar.
- Strong interest in data and data analytics, specifically in construction cost data and schedule information.
- Computer literacy essential. Working knowledge of advanced modelling, Python, Power BI, common data environments (CDEs) and experience of AI and AI data analysis is highly preferable.
- Analytical skills essential, combined with good written and oral communication.
- A solid understanding of the preconstruction process is an added plus.
- Strong diplomacy and influencing skills to communicate a position or concern regarding project cost or timing.
- Proactive, strategic and critical thinker.
- Knowledge of European languages, as well as English, is an advantage.
