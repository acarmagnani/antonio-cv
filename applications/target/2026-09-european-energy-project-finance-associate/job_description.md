# Associate, Project Finance — European Energy

**Company:** European Energy A/S
**Location:** Søborg / Copenhagen, Denmark
**Team:** Project and Asset Finance (CCO Area), part of a 25+ person M&A and Project Finance department
**Reference:** Jeremy Bennett, Director, Global Head of Project and Asset Finance
**Posted:** 2026-09

Looking for a role where you can build your project finance career while providing critical support to live, high-value transactions? Join our Project Finance team in Copenhagen as an Associate.

European Energy is a growing company in the renewable energy sector. We are a privately owned Danish company founded in 2004, with our headquarters in Copenhagen. We develop, construct, finance, and operate solar (PV) and wind energy projects globally, as well as Power-to-X projects. In addition, we explore new technologies, including battery storage, biogas, and carbon capture. Our core values form the foundation of everything we do: In It Together, Trust and Courage, Ambition, Excitement, Inclusion, and Agility.

## Your New Team

As a part of the CCO Area, the Project and Asset Finance team is responsible for sourcing all financing needed to construct EE's project pipeline. In this role, you will work directly with the Senior Associate and wider transaction team, as well as the more than 25 other members of the broader M&A and Project Finance department dedicated to commercialising EE's asset portfolio.

## Your New Role

As an Associate, Project Finance you will provide hands-on support to the transaction team, working closely with the Senior Associate and wider deal team. You will become well-versed in the financial model and able to run scenarios and sensitivities, while supporting the team wherever needed. This is an excellent entry point into project finance, with direct exposure to live, high-value transactions from day one.

## Your Responsibilities

- Provide ad-hoc support to the transaction team as directed.
- Become well-versed in the financial model and able to run scenarios and sensitivities.
- Support the Senior Associate in maintaining the dataroom and coordinating lender/advisor Q&A.
- Assist in preparing transaction information materials.
- Support due diligence and documentation processes as needed.

## Prerequisites to progress in the job

- You have 0-2 years of relevant experience, for example from a graduate programme (with relevant coursework), internship, or junior analyst role in finance, infrastructure, or a related field.
- You have strong quantitative and Excel skills, and an aptitude for financial modelling.
- You have an interest in developing a career in project finance and renewable energy.
- You are structured, thorough, and comfortable picking up new tasks quickly.

As a person, you are curious and eager to learn, with a structured approach to your work and an eye for detail. You are collaborative and communicate clearly, and you are motivated by the opportunity to build your project finance skills on live transactions from day one.

You may occasionally be asked to travel to meet with stakeholders across Europe.
