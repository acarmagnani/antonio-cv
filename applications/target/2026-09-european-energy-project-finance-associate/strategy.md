# Positioning, European Energy, Associate Project Finance

**One line:** An analyst who has already sized investments and tested viability on real assets, and who has worked on capital-intensive energy and water projects to the appraisal standards development-bank lenders require, entering project finance at exactly the rung the posting describes.

**Profile chosen:** `profile.real-estate-investment`. The posting's hard requirement is quantitative (financial model, scenarios and sensitivities, Excel), so the profile has to lead with feasibility, financial modeling and investment sizing rather than with sustainability. Sector credibility in renewables comes from the Sweco bullets immediately below it, not from the profile line.

**Threads to lead with:**

1. **Lender-facing infrastructure work at Sweco.** Capital-intensive water, energy and transport assets financed by the World Bank, EBRD, KfW and EIB, worked to the appraisal and reporting standards those lenders require, plus asset-level climate risk rated to inform investment decisions and engineering design review under FIDIC. This is the closest thing in his record to sitting on the sponsor side of a financed infrastructure project, and it is what makes lender Q&A, due diligence and documentation support familiar rather than new.

2. **Financial modelling and Excel he has actually built.** Financial feasibility studies at OSPA sizing investment and return against buildable area, unit mix and price per square metre; a pricing tool at Sweco parametrising fee bands and project economics; a fully automated risk-assessment system in Excel at Ramboll; an Excel and VBA financial analysis tool for a private client. The point is not that he has run a project finance model, it is that he builds and works inside quantitative models and is quick in Excel, which is what a 0-2 year seat asks for.

**Honest gap, do not paper over it:** no project finance modelling (debt sizing, DSCR, gearing), no live transaction. The posting asks for aptitude and interest rather than deal experience, so the letter should show the aptitude concretely and state the interest in renewables plainly, never claim the deal experience.
