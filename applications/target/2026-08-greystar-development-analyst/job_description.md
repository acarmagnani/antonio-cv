# Development Analyst — Greystar

Location: Netherlands (Dutch preferred, not required; English essential). Greystar is a global residential real estate investor, developer and operator, active in multifamily (build-to-rent) and student housing (PBSA).

## Role

The Development Analyst supports the execution of Greystar's development platform by performing (financial) analysis of development opportunities, preparing information and documents to facilitate the underwriting and closing of real estate transactions, interfacing with land developers, brokers, equity partners, institutional clients and Greystar's leadership, and tracking and reporting on the status of multifamily (BTR) and student (PBSA) transactions within the assigned geographic markets.

## Key role responsibilities

- Conduct (financial) analysis of development opportunities, including detailed pro-forma and financial models. Gather, compile, investigate and analyse market data to identify potential development opportunities, and prepare narrative development memoranda, detailed reports and market summaries with insights on proposed acquisitions.
- Establish and maintain productive relationships with local land developers, market brokers, institutional and equity partners, lenders, real estate owners and other key stakeholders, managing ongoing communication on proposed development transactions.
- Maintain communications within the development team and with business leaders on the project, providing periodic updates on progress against programme and budget, resolving issues that may compromise delivery schedule or costs.
- Identify, investigate and refer potential acquisitions and other real estate transactions to Greystar leadership, and assist in preparing financial, market and other documents required for new business pitches and RFPs.
- Organise, lead and participate in due diligence on properties under contract, developing documentation and recommendations on key findings that may affect the return and financial performance of the proposed deal.
- Monitor and report on the status of proposed development transactions in the assigned region, establish critical timelines and deadlines, and address issues that could adversely affect closing.
- Complete human resources, financial, administrative and other reports and analysis.
- Development management support on the acquisition process with commercial and technical development knowledge, design processes from initial design through realisation, collaborations with partners, suppliers and contractors, project reporting on progress, budget and timelines, legal documentation and agreements with municipalities, advisors and contractors, and setting up initial development budgets.

## About you

- A degree in a business, analytical or related field.
- Advanced Excel and PowerPoint skills.
- Some analytical experience within real estate, through internships or previous development roles.
- Fluency in English essential, fluency in Dutch preferred.
- Proven exposure to financial modelling, valuations and analysis, ideally with knowledge of multifamily (BTR), student accommodation (PBSA) or related sectors.
- An interest in building regulations and planning processes.
- Highly numerate with excellent attention to detail, a confident communicator able to turn complex data into clear reports and presentations.
- Commercially minded, naturally curious team player who thrives under pressure and builds positive relationships.
