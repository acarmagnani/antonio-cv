# Positioning: Ørsted, Strategic Projects

**One line:** someone who has done consulting work in two engineering and management consultancies on energy and water infrastructure, structuring uneven evidence into recommendations that clients and lenders acted on, applying to do the same thing inside the company rather than for it.

**Profile chosen:** `profile.infrastructure-consulting`, overriding the block's ESG profile. Ørsted is an energy company, and this profile names international infrastructure consulting on water and energy projects, work from tender through delivery, and technical analysis. The ESG profile would have positioned him as a sustainability specialist, which is a narrower and less accurate read of what this team does.

**Threads to lead with**

1. **Consulting delivered, not observed.** Sweco supplies development-bank-financed energy, water and transport work, climate risk assessments written to JASPERS and EBRD standards, the technical and methodology sections of competitive tenders, and a pricing tool that parametrised expert fee bands and project economics so bid decisions rested on figures. Ramboll supplies corporate-client due diligence, an automated risk assessment system, and the critical raw materials market study that turned regulatory pressure into a list of business development targets. Those are business cases and structured problem-solving under other names.
2. **Stakeholders who owed him nothing.** The Baku field missions put him with national water agencies and government counterparts to align on rehabilitation priorities and extract technical data. OSPA put him in front of developer clients presenting feasibility outcomes they were deciding on. This is the closest evidence to the stakeholder engagement the posting puts at its centre.

**The honest gap:** no top-tier strategy consultancy on the CV, no project scoped and driven through to implementation under his own name, and no executive-level storyline prepared for a governance forum. The published salary band confirms the level is above him. The posting invites transferable-skill applications explicitly, and this application takes that invitation at face value rather than pretending the gap is not there.

**Bucket:** `target/`. Energy and consulting is the adjacent family where his odds are genuinely best, and an in-house consulting seat at Ørsted has internal mobility into most of the company.
