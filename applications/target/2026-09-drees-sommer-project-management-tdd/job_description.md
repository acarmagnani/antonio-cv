# Project Management & Technical Due Diligence — Drees & Sommer, Stockholm

**Company:** Drees & Sommer (Dreso), 6,500+ employees at 80+ locations, consulting and delivery for real estate, industry, energy and infrastructure
**Location:** Stockholm, Sweden
**Sectors:** logistics, data centres, hospitality, commercial developments

---

## Job description

As part of the team in Stockholm, you will support the delivery of real estate and construction projects across sectors such as logistics, data centres, hospitality and commercial developments. Combining project management and Technical Due Diligence (TDD), you will work closely with experienced colleagues, coordinate project activities, contribute to technical assessments, and help create structure and transparency throughout the project lifecycle.

The role offers the opportunity to build a broad understanding of the real estate and construction industry while developing both technical and commercial capabilities. As you grow in the role, you will gradually take on greater responsibility, manage your own workstreams, and become a valued contact for clients and project stakeholders.

- Supporting real estate and construction projects from early planning through successful delivery.
- Coordinating technical, commercial and organizational work packages in project management and technical due diligence.
- Preparing project documentation, reports, feasibility inputs and decision-making materials for clients and internal project teams.
- Tracking schedules, costs, quality requirements, risks and opportunities within your area of responsibility.
- Supporting communication with clients, consultants and project partners on selected project topics.
- Developing your project management expertise while taking on clearly defined responsibilities in project delivery.

## Qualifications

- Degree in Civil Engineering, Architecture, Construction Management, Real Estate, or a related field.
- Initial practical experience in a project-based environment, ideally within construction, real estate, consulting, or technical project management, is an advantage.
- Interest in interdisciplinary project work, consulting, and client advisory services.
- Strong analytical skills and proficiency in MS Office applications.
- **Fluency in Swedish (C1 level or higher) and English, both written and spoken.**
- Structured, proactive and collaborative working style, with strong communication skills and a willingness to gradually take on increased responsibility.
