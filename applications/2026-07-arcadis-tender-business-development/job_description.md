# Tender & Business Development Specialist (f/m/d) — Arcadis (Spain)

Posting language: English. Required languages: English + Spanish. Portuguese a valued asset.

## Company

We are more than 34,000 people, in over 30 countries, dedicated to improving quality of life. Everyone has an important role to play. With the power of many curious minds, together we can solve the world's most complex challenges and deliver more impact together.

## Role description

As Tender & Business Development Specialist (f/m/d), you will play a key role in the coordination, preparation and submission of technical and commercial proposals for both public and private clients. You will support the full bid lifecycle, from tender opportunity identification and tender document review through proposal preparation, coordination of contributors and final submission, ensuring compliance, quality and timely delivery.

## Role accountabilities

- Coordinate and support the entire tender process, ensuring timely and accurate preparation of technical, administrative and commercial documentation.
- Review and analyse tender documentation, specifications, contractual requirements and client instructions for both public and private clients, identifying key obligations, risks and submission requirements.
- Organise, prioritise and manage multiple bid requests simultaneously, coordinating activities across different opportunities, deadlines and stakeholders.
- Support bid/no-bid assessments and contribute to tender planning and strategy discussions.
- Coordinate internal contributors (technical teams, project managers, commercial teams and support functions) to obtain the information required for proposal development.
- Prepare, compile and quality-check tender submissions, ensuring compliance with client requirements and internal standards.
- Analyse and summarise technical information, translating complex project inputs into clear, structured and client-focused proposal content.
- Maintain document control and organised file management throughout the bidding process.
- Provide support to the Commercial Enablement and Business Development teams with bid-related information, reporting and data management.
- Support the continuous improvement of bidding processes, tools, templates and knowledge-sharing practices.
- Monitor tender KPIs and maintain bid databases for reporting and performance tracking.
- Ensure consistency, quality and punctual delivery of all proposal submissions.
- Work effectively within an international and multidisciplinary environment, collaborating with local and global teams.
- Demonstrate strong ownership, organisation and prioritisation skills across multiple concurrent opportunities.
- Adopt and promote the Arcadis Way and Arcadis core values in day-to-day activities.

## Qualifications & Experience

- Degree in Architecture, Civil Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Environmental Engineering or another technical discipline.

Candidates with experience in engineering, construction, architecture or consultancy environments, including the preparation and coordination of public and private tenders, review of tender documentation and management of multidisciplinary proposal submissions, will be particularly valued.
- Proficiency in English and Spanish is required. Portuguese is not mandatory but would be considered a valuable asset.
