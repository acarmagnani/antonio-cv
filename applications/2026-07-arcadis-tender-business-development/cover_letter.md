Dear Hiring Team at Arcadis,

I trained as an architect in Brazil, and the part of the work that held me was never the drawings but the step before them, where it gets decided which projects are worth pursuing and how the case for them is made. That interest pulled me toward the commercial and strategic side of the built environment, and eventually to Denmark for an M.Arch. in Architecture Computation and then into international infrastructure consulting. The Tender & Business Development Specialist role at Arcadis, where a single bid has to hold together across disciplines, countries, and both public and private clients, is where I want to take that further.

At Sweco Danmark I work on internationally funded water, energy and transport projects financed by the World Bank, EBRD, KfW and EIB, and a large part of that is bid work. I write the technical and methodology sections of tender proposals, articulating how the firm would actually deliver the project, and I assemble bid teams by sourcing specialist consultants who can evidence the local and technical expertise a tender demands. I also built the pricing tool behind our bid decisions, parametrizing expert fee bands and project economics so different tender configurations can be tested for financial viability before anyone commits. The job comes down to reading a tender document for what it really requires, gathering the right people and evidence against a deadline, and submitting something that is both compliant and commercially sound.

The technical review side comes from the same place. I check engineering designs under FIDIC Yellow Book arrangements against subconsultants' drawings, and earlier at Itaú-Unibanco I reviewed architecture and engineering documentation against Brazilian building, accessibility and fire-safety norms before construction could be authorized. At Ramboll I led a market study mapping 20+ critical raw materials supply chains to identify prospective clients and new service lines, connecting regulatory drivers to concrete business development targets. I work in English, Spanish and Portuguese.

Arcadis runs this work across 30 countries and the full spread of built-environment disciplines, and the commercial side of infrastructure is the direction I want my career to take.

All the best,
Antonio Carmagnani
