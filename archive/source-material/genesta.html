Key Documents and Resources for Genesta Suppliers

This page provides a set of documents to help Genesta's business partners understand and meet our expectations on human rights, labor conditions, and responsible business conduct. Whether you are a contractor, subcontractor, or service provider, these resources will guide you in aligning your daily operations with international standards and Genesta's Code of Conduct.
Use these materials to:
  • Strengthen your company's practices on site;
  • Train teams and subcontractors;
  • Clarify your responsibilities when working with Genesta.
