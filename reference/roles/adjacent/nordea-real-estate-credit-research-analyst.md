## Job opening

Senior Real Estate Analyst, Credit Research, Nordea Markets, Stockholm

Would you like to utilise your analytical skills and still have a lot of investor interactions? We are now looking for a Credit Research Analyst for our Nordic LC&I Markets team.

About Our Team

Welcome to the Nordea Credit Research team. We add value by closely analysing inaugural and recurring issuers in the capital market; assessing risks and opportunities to credit investors both in fundamental credit research and risk/reward-based investment opportunities. We are now looking for a Senior Analyst to play a valuable role in Nordea's very successful credit franchise and will be part of the highly ranked Credit Research team.

What you'll be doing:

As the Senior Analyst, you'll cover a portfolio of corporate issuers mainly within but not limited to the real estate and construction sector, drawing on potential industry knowledge.
We expect you to take responsibility for the Credit Research product, develop our offering and act as a local franchise ambassador.
We expect you to be eager to continue to learn, have an ability to work independently and do not shy away from neither workload nor challenges.

You'll join a highly dedicated and ambitious Nordic team with specialists within credit research. The role is based in Stockholm, Sweden. Fluency in Swedish or other Scandinavian language and English is a prerequisite. Some travel in the Nordics is to be expected.

Who You Are

Collaboration. Ownership. Passion. Courage. These are the values that guide us in being at our best, and that we imagine you share with us.

For this position, you can either have solid knowledge of the financial dynamics for corporates, or you might already have a strong reach to Nordic and European credit investors.

To succeed in this role, we believe that you:

Are goal oriented with a commercial attitude and motivation to excel.
Are positive, proactive and work well in teams, also under pressure.
Work independently with integrity and ownership.
Have an ability to handle multiple projects without compromising quality.
Show great communication and presentation skills.
Have a performance-driven mindset and strive to deliver results that surpass expectations, even when the deadlines are tight.
Will support innovation and our continuous journey towards an efficient and modern research product.

We expect the Senior Analyst to have:

Strong academic record with master's Degree within Finance, Economics or similar.
5-10 years of relevant work experience.
Strong industrial knowledge in the real estate industry.
Excellent knowledge about the capital market dynamics and pricing mechanisms.
Excellent financial modelling skills.
Great analytical- and financial skills.
Basic programming skills that support quantitative analysis, data handling and research automation or an willingness to learn.
